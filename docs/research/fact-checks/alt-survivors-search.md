# Search verification: "The smaller surviving social worlds of 2026 and new entrants"

Verified 2026-10-09 against the brief at scratchpad/research/alt-survivors.md.

## Method note

- 10 of 10 WebSearch calls used (one per claim, standard mode). Page fetching (WebFetch/curl) is blocked in this sandbox, so every verdict rests on the quoted extracts the search tool returned, not on reading the pages. Where an extract did not mention a sub-fact, that sub-fact is noted as "not in extract".
- Steam concurrency figures are from third-party trackers (Steambase, tracker.gg, live-player-count) and are snapshots two to three months old (latest late July / 19 Aug 2026).

## Additional findings

- Palia expanded to PS5 and Xbox with Royal Highlands (12 May 2026); the 10m-player announcement came from EG7 (Enad Global 7), Daybreak's listed parent, on 22 April 2026.
- Pax Dei monetisation is buy-to-play plus an optional subscription required to own land plots: a direct "rent for your home" precedent for the nolife design implication.
- Hytale early access is sold only through hytale.com, so no Steam concurrency data exists for it; the brief's Steam-floor caveat does not apply.
- Avakin Life's Steam reception is Mixed (63% of 968 reviews) with controls and monetisation as the main complaints; the 500m figure is mobile-only per Notebookcheck.
- NeosVR's Steam store page carries the "no longer sold" notice; local/LAN/VLAN play still works, so it is a frozen offline client rather than fully dead.
- Spatial is refunding web subscriptions within 14 days of 27 Jul 2026; mobile/VR subs had to be cancelled via app stores.
- Not covered by budget: Lockwood 2023 financials (Preqin), Palia summer 2026 updates, Hytale Chapter 1 preview, Pax Dei mid-2026 CCU, Sansar late-2021 outage, Resonite non-Steam launcher usage.
