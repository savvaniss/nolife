## Additional findings

- Hypergrid Business May 15, 2026 report: 149,536 regions and 48,376 actives across public OpenSim; OSgrid at 4,420 actives (vs 3,931 in Jan 2026), so OSgrid was reporting stats in May 2026 before the June outage. Registered users fell ~15,000 because EdMondo stopped reporting. https://www.hypergridbusiness.com/2026/05/opensim-users-land-area-both-up-this-month/
- Jan 2026 grid ranking: Wolf Territories Grid led with 11,708 actives; OSgrid second with 3,931.
- There is a separate HB item "OSgrid enters immediate long-term maintenance" (https://hypergridbusiness.com/?p=78935) preceding the June 15 piece; no source found confirming OSgrid reopened after June 21, 2026.
- CCUG week 33 (Aug 14, 2026) notes list "building out the foundations in terms of glTF 2.0 specification support", blend shapes and custom rigs; no mention of OpenUSD. Week 27 also hinted LL is "leaning back toward Vulkan" and has no plans to extend prims.
- Project Zero's Avatar Picker was ported to SL Mobile; Lab says lessons feed desktop viewer and mobile. A streamed Firestorm variant was also run and was short-lived.
- AOUSD Core Spec 1.1 targets animation-spline feature parity with OpenUSD 25.08. New July members joined at General Member level (working-group voice, no steering seat).
- VRChat credits-to-USD: 30,000 Credits = $150 on the welcome page (so 1 credit = $0.005; a 1,200-credit floor = $6 at that rate, though the FAQ says ~$10, implying rates/floors have changed over time).
