## Verdicts by claim

### Claim 0 — CONFIRMED

> Roblox made facial age estimation (via Persona) required to access chat globally from January 2026, sorting users into six bands (under 9, 9–12, 13–15, 16–17, 18–20, 21+) with chat limited by default to adjacent bands; enforcement began December 2025 in Australia, New Zealand and the Netherlands.

**Note.** Roblox's 7 Jan 2026 release (BusinessWire/Nasdaq republication) confirms: Persona facial age estimation or ID check (13+) required for chat; six bands under 9 / 9-12 / 13-15 / 16-17 / 18-20 / 21+; default chat only with the band directly above and below; chat off for under-9 without parental consent; December 2025 enforcement in Australia, New Zealand and the Netherlands. Nuance: January rollout began with US users and reached all other chat regions 'over the following week'; Roblox says bands may vary by region/product and that it will ask users to re-run the check when behaviour suggests a different age. Images are processed by Persona and deleted after estimation.

**Source.** https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/01/roblox-age-checks-required-to-chat

### Claim 4 — CORRECTED

> The European Commission published DSA Article 28 guidelines on protection of minors and a prototype privacy-preserving age-verification app on 14 July 2025, applying to all platforms accessible to minors (not just VLOPs) and treating self-declared age as unreliable.

**Note.** Date (14 Jul 2025), prototype age-verification app, and 'self-declaration insufficient' confirmed (CMS; Digital Poland; Inside Privacy). Corrections: the guidelines apply to platforms accessible to minors EXCEPT micro and small enterprises, and are non-binding (Commission will use them as a 'significant and meaningful' benchmark). Verification is expected for adult content and high-risk environments; estimation is recommended only where verification is disproportionate. The app is a pilot/stopgap until the EU Digital Identity Wallet (expected end-2026); risk reviews must be repeated at least annually.

**Source.** https://cms.law/en/fra/news-information/protecting-minors-in-the-digital-space

### Claim 9 — CORRECTED

> The EU AI Act Article 50 transparency duties (machine-readable marking by providers; perceptible deepfake labels by deployers) apply from 2 August 2026, with final Commission guidelines published 20 July 2026 and about 190 signatories to the voluntary Code of Practice by end of July 2026.

**Note.** Confirmed: final Article 50 guidelines issued 20 Jul 2026 (Stephenson Harwood), Code of Practice published June 2026 with 'about 190' signatories by end July 2026 (Commission digital-strategy page; MoFo 11 Sep 2026), obligations applying from 2 Aug 2026. Correction/nuance: under the Digital Omnibus on AI (political agreement May 2026; Council approval reported 29 Jun 2026, OJ publication not confirmed) the Art 50(2) machine-readable marking duty for generative systems already on the market before 2 Aug 2026 is deferred to 2 Dec 2026 — not Feb 2027 as the earlier checkers assumed. Guidelines accept plain-language notices, persistent badges or spoken statements but not a generic 'assistant' label or buried T&Cs; AI agents must say on whose behalf they act; content generated before 2 Aug 2026 need not be relabelled.

## Additional findings (for designing a successor world)

- Method: WebFetch/curl were blocked, so every verdict rests on search-engine extracts of the cited pages, not full text; 12 of 12 searches used; claims 2 (AVS/£18m cap), 6 (24,522 reports), 7 (Personal Boundary) and 8 (Steam ban, SEC 2025 stance) were only partly searched and lean on prior-checker knowledge for the unsearched halves.
- The Roblox state settlements have converged on a contractual template a successor world will be measured against: age assurance for all users, under-18 chat restricted by default to the same or adjacent age band, adult-to-under-16 contact only via verified trusted connections, safe-content mode by default for under-16s, with liquidated damages on breach (Kansas $10m; South Dakota up to $5.4m extra) and a most-favoured-nation clause for Alabama that propagates stricter terms across states.
- Roblox now re-runs age checks when behavioural signals suggest a user is much older or younger than their band, and says bands may vary by region/product — age assurance is a continuous process, not a one-off gate.
- Roblox's Q2 2026 10-Q (as of 30 Jul 2026) lists settled states as Alabama, Mississippi, Nevada, South Dakota and West Virginia and says it is negotiating with others; Kansas followed 29 Sep 2026. South Dakota's package is reported as $15m over four years ($8.6m guaranteed), so the brief's '$54m' total should be given as a range until Mississippi's amount is found.
- Ofcom's age-assurance orders carry daily penalties (£1,000/day for 8579 LLC, £200-250/day for Kick) and a duty to disclose a full list of operated sites; the 31 Jul 2025 investigation covered 34 sites across four operators, so enforcement is portfolio-wide per operator rather than per site.
- eSafety's not-restricted status is advisory: unless the Government prescribes platforms, whether a service is an 'age-restricted social media platform' is ultimately for a court, and Discord, Roblox and Steam had to file written self-assessments with evidence — a successor world should keep a documented primary-purpose self-assessment ready.
- The EU age-verification app is explicitly a stopgap until the EU Digital Identity Wallet (expected end-2026); the DSA minors guidelines also expect a documented risk review at least annually and on significant design changes, with features classified low/medium/high risk.
- Discord breach: beyond ID photos, the vendor ticketing system exposed last-four card digits, IPs and full support transcripts, and the intrusion was reportedly social-engineered via a phone call to a support agent; vendor identity (5CA vs 'a Zendesk instance') and dates remain contested in press accounts.
- Sentinel reportedly processes ~6 billion chat messages a day in one-minute snapshots and has run since late 2024; the '35% caught proactively' figure could not be found outside Roblox's own post, so treat it as company-reported.
- Meta's Horizon Worlds exit ended VR world building/publishing on 15 Jun 2026 and was justified by >4x mobile MAU growth in 2025 amid ~10% Reality Labs layoffs; the social-VR market for a successor world is therefore smaller and less contested, but the harassment evidence base it generated remains the reference set regulators cite.
- AI Act Art 50 guidelines (20 Jul 2026) set concrete disclosure forms for AI characters/NPCs: plain-language notice, persistent badge or spoken statement, never a generic 'assistant' label or T&C-only disclosure; autonomous agents must state on whose behalf they act; extra care is expected where children are in the audience; pre-2 Aug 2026 content needs no retro-labelling; marking duty for pre-existing generative systems deferred to 2 Dec 2026 under the Digital Omnibus.
