## Detail

**Claim 0 - confirmed.** Andrew Chen's post (summarising Massively): "New-user retention to 90 days is still about 10% according to Linden Lab, having apparently remained more or less unchanged over the last 12 months." Neville Hobson's 29 Jan 2007 post cites "10 percent at 90 days" via Tateru Nino. BetaNews derived ~320,000 everyday users from ~3.2M accounts on that basis. These are secondhand reports of Linden numbers.

**Claim 1 - confirmed.** The jerz.setonhill.edu copy of Frank Rose's "How Madison Avenue Is Wasting Millions on a Deserted Second Life" (Wired, July 2007) reproduces all the numbers: >85% of avatars abandoned; ~7M publicised avatars vs ~4M distinct individuals, ~1M logged in in the previous 30 days; Sears store on IBM Innovation Island traffic 281, Coke Virtual Thirst 27; Money Island 136,000 and Sexy Beach 133,000 as top locations.

**Claim 2 - corrected.** Linden's Q4 2008/2008 release confirms nearly 400M user hours (+61%), a record 112M in Q4, and Q4 2008 peak concurrency 76,000 (+6.7% over Q3, ~30% over Q4 2007). The US$361M user-to-user figure appears only in PC World, not in Linden's own release, and the +54% growth PC World cited was Linden's Q4-vs-Q4 figure; Linden's own annual money figure is LindeX exchange volume of US$108M (+33%). The all-time peak concurrency is given by Wikipedia as 88,200 in Q1 2009 (the ledger's "88,000 (2009)" is approximately right but the quarter matters). The "Q3 2009 >77,000 after bot-restriction policy" detail was not found in any extract; Linden's Q2 2009 metrics post did discuss bot-policy effects on hours and logins. Recommend: keep 400M hours and 76k; flag US$361M as PC World-only; cite 88,200 Q1 2009; drop or tag the Q3 2009 figure as unverified.

**Claim 3 - confirmed.** Game Developer / Engadget / TechCrunch: early-June 2010 restructuring cut 30% of staff; on Thursday 24 June 2010 Kingdon stepped down, Rosedale (chairman) became interim CEO, CFO Bob Komin added COO. The "300+ staff" headcount and "merged product and engineering" wording were not in the extracts (likely from The Register, which was in the ledger). Rod Humble was named CEO in December 2010.

**Claim 4 - confirmed.** Hypergrid Business (Sept 2013) quotes Renderosity: "Renderosity products can not be used in Second Life for any reason", and names CG Textures among sites forbidding uploads; it also notes Linden's spokesman said the content-grab reading was a misunderstanding but the ToS was not changed. Linden's community blog "Updates to Section 2.3 of the Terms of Service" and modemworld confirm the revision on Wednesday 16 July 2014; the revised 5th paragraph reads "...with respect to Second Life, Inworld or otherwise on the Service as permitted by you through your interactions with the Service." The "affirmative user action" paraphrase is consistent with that wording. Attorney Agenda Faromet presented the change to the SL Bar Association on 2 Aug 2014.

**Claim 5 - confirmed, with nuance.** SL wiki: initial Zindra map had 157 regions, significantly expanded 3-10 July 2009; ~850 move tickets submitted 29 Jun-24 Jul 2009; remaining land auctioned from 8 Sep 2009; after 15 Sep 2009 publicly promoted adult content had to be on Zindra or private Adult regions. Viewer 1.23 "Adult enabled" and Zindra opened 15 June 2009 (Engadget 16 June, "a few days earlier than expected"). Nuance: 15 Sep 2009 is the content-placement deadline, not a move-ticket deadline (the ticket window closed 24 July). The 12 March 2009 announcement date did not appear in extracts.

**Claim 6 - corrected.** Reuters bureau announced in early/mid October 2006 (NBC/journalism.co.uk; bit-tech 16 Oct 2006), Adam Pasick as "Adam Reuters": confirmed. Second House of Sweden opened 30 May 2007 by Carl Bildt, replica of the Washington embassy, second after the Maldives: confirmed. Corrections: November 2008 is when the closure became public (The Baron 21 Nov; Press Gazette 25 Nov, citing The Register that Pasick "hadn't filed a story in months"); CSMonitor says it was quietly shuttered in September 2008 and MediaShift says October 2008. The 400,000 SEK cost rests on a single blog (kullin.net); no official Swedish Institute figure surfaced. Recommend: "closed quietly in autumn 2008 (reported November 2008)" and attribute the cost figure.

**Claim 7 - confirmed.** THE Journal (3 Nov 2010): the new policy "would essentially double the normal region rates" effective 1 Jan 2011, with pre-paid 6/12/18/24-month contracts offered to keep the discount; the notice went by email, not blog. Hypergrid Business (Aug 2013) and modemworld confirm the 50% discount's return, announced Wednesday 24 July 2013, covering private-region set-up and maintenance for accredited institutions and 501(c)(3)s, though not on identical terms. The "drove institutions toward OpenSim" effect was not directly in the extracts.

**Claim 8 - confirmed.** SL wiki: origins 21 Sept 2004 as the Neualtenburg Project (Bavarian theme, Anzere sim); moved to a private region and renamed Neufreistadt in 2005; renamed CDS by referendum; a six-region microcontinent (A230); ~80 citizens, constant over the decades; celebrated its second decade on 21 Sept 2024. The land store confirms six regions and "land ownership = citizenship with the right to vote and run for office". The three-person Executive / five-member Representative Assembly / five-member Scientific Council (with Dean) structure comes from the Steamlands fan wiki, which is out of date (lists three sims), so current body sizes are unconfirmed; the ledger's "medium" confidence is appropriate.

**Claim 9 - unverifiable.** The search returned no extract containing 49,798, 26,663, ~9,033 or 68.98M; Daniel Voyager's 2025 posts did not surface and could not be fetched. Nearest datapoints found: 27,334 regions in mid-February 2022 and >70M registered accounts (webtribunal, citing Voyager). The figures remain third-party-tracker claims resting on the researcher's original search extract.

## Additional findings
- All-time peak concurrency per Wikipedia: 88,200 in Q1 2009.
- Linden's own 2008 money figure: LindeX exchange volume US$108M (+33%); the +54% U2U growth was Q4-over-Q4.
- BetaNews (2007): ~320,000 everyday users implied by 10% retention on ~3.2M accounts; comScore estimated 1.28M actives.
- Reuters' bureau closure was quiet (Sept or Oct 2008); November 2008 is only when it was noticed.
- CDS: ~80 citizens; 20th anniversary 21 Sept 2024.
- Restored 2013 education discount announced 24 July 2013; educators reported wary of it.
- SL Bar Association presentation on the July 2014 ToS and Skill Gaming policy, 2 Aug 2014.
- Rod Humble named CEO December 2010.
