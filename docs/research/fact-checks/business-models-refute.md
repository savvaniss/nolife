## Additional findings (from the checker's recollection; verify before use)

1. Roblox FY2024 average DAU is likely ~82.9M, not 79.5M; ABPDAU is therefore ~$52-53, slightly below the brief's ~$55.
2. Roblox's own "where each dollar goes" disclosure (developer economics page) gives creators roughly 24-29c per dollar after ~24c of app-store fees and ~26c of platform hosting/support; the brief's figure matches but should cite that page directly.
3. Reality Labs cumulative losses pass $70B only when 2019 or 2025 quarters are counted; for an investor document, quote FY totals and the exact window.
4. For VCOIN, the regulatory condition set (transfer caps per user, KYC at redemption, no promotion as investment) is more relevant to a successor's currency design than the wind-down date; read the letter's conditions, not just its existence.
5. The Fortnite 40% pool is shared with Epic's own modes; press reports put creator islands at ~35-40% of playtime, so creators' realised share of net revenue is closer to 15-20%.
6. Every one of these numbers predates or straddles 2025-2026 changes (Roblox RDC 2025 economics changes and regional pricing, Epic's in-island item sales from Dec 2025, Linden Lab 2024-2025 pricing and mobile launch); the brief should be re-run with live web access before any figure is used in the nolife plan.
