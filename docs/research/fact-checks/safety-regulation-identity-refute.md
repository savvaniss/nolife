## Additional findings the researcher missed (relevant to designing a successor world)

1. **App-store-level age verification is arriving regardless of platform choices.** Utah's App Store Accountability Act (effective May 2025) and Texas's version (SB 2420, scheduled 1 Jan 2026 but preliminarily enjoined by a federal court in Dec 2025) push age categories and parental consent to Apple/Google; Apple's Declared Age Range API already exposes an age band to apps. A virtual world distributed via mobile stores will inherit an age band from the store and must be able to consume it.
2. **The DSA minors guidelines exempt micro/small enterprises** (unless VLOP) and are non-binding; a small studio launching in the EU is not immediately bound, but the guidelines are the standard a DSC will apply once it grows.
3. **Private litigation, not only AG suits, is the bigger Roblox exposure**: hundreds of individual grooming/CSAM suits were centralised into a federal MDL (N.D. Cal., Dec 2025), and several name Discord as co-defendant — cross-platform contact (world → Discord DM) is the alleged harm pathway. Design implication: treat off-platform contact prompts ("add me on Discord") as a detection signal.
4. **Discord's breach vector was the appeals/BPO vendor (5CA via Zendesk)**, and the breach also exposed names, emails, partial payment data and support transcripts — i.e. the support ticketing system, not the age-estimation model, is the crown-jewel surface.
5. **Meta's Personal Boundary became user-adjustable (off/2ft/4ft) in March 2022**; the lesson is "default-on, user-controllable", not "mandatory".
6. **Ofcom's first OSA penalties were for ignoring information requests** (4chan, Oct 2025; Itai Tech, Nov 2025), reinforcing that a regulator-response function is a day-one requirement.
7. **EU Digital Omnibus (Nov 2025)** proposes delaying AI Act Art 50(2) marking for pre-existing generative systems to Feb 2027 and other simplifications; the AI-provenance timeline is politically fluid.
8. **Australian penalties and scope creep**: up to A$49.5m per breach for platforms; eSafety has said the platform list is reassessed continuously and that gaming platforms with substantial social features can be re-designated — framing as a "game" is a mitigation, not a guarantee.
