## Additional findings (things the brief omits that matter for designing a successor)

These are from the checker's prior knowledge and are flagged with the same unverifiable status; they are offered as leads, not facts.

1. **Roblox is the control group the brief lacks.** The one UGC virtual world that scaled through the same period (DAU in the 80M-100M+ range by 2025, with creator payouts reported in the high hundreds of millions per year) is mentioned only as a shape ("Roblox-likes"). A postmortem of why Horizon/Rec Room/Sandbox failed is incomplete without the comparison: Roblox is mobile-first, ships its own engine and economy, keeps a large platform share of each transaction, and its growth came from under-16s. Rec Room's 30c-vs-70c disclosure should be read against Roblox's publicly filed take-rate and developer-exchange economics.
2. **Fortnite/UEFN (Creator Economy 2.0, launched March 2023)** is the second omitted comparator: Epic pays creators from a pooled 40% of net revenue by engagement rather than per-item sales, which is a structurally different answer to the UGC-margin problem the brief identifies in Rec Room.
3. **Second Life itself did not die.** Linden Lab reported the world remained profitable with roughly 1M MAU through the 2020s and launched an official mobile client (beta 2024-25). For a successor to "nolife", the fact that a 2003 world with a user-owned economy outlived Horizon, AltspaceVR, Mesh and (per claim 6) Rec Room is itself the strongest datum: durable virtual worlds have been economy-first and user-owned, not hardware-first.
4. **Meta's January 2026 Reality Labs cuts** (reported ~10% of the division, closure of several first-party VR studios, shift toward wearables/AI glasses) are referenced in the brief's source list but not in its findings; they are the proximate cause of the March 2026 Horizon VR decision in claim 2 and should be tied together.
5. **Meta's Horizon Engine / mobile pivot (announced at Connect, Sept 2025)** replaced Unity as the world runtime; this is what stranded Unity-built VR worlds and is the mechanism behind the "existing Unity worlds vs new Horizon Engine worlds" split in claim 2. The design lesson (engine migrations strand UGC) is in the brief but the causal chain is not spelled out.
6. **Smart-glasses, not headsets, became the hardware story in 2025-26** (Ray-Ban Meta volumes in the millions; Meta Ray-Ban Display, Sept 2025). A successor world planning for "best graphics of the era" should note that the mass-market wearable is a non-immersive glasses form factor, which argues for the brief's cross-platform conclusion from a different direction.
7. **Measurement standard exists to borrow:** the DappRadar-vs-Decentraland fight (claim 4) can be resolved by adopting the game-industry standard (DAU = authenticated session with >N minutes of input), which Roblox and Second Life already publish. The brief recommends a public metric but does not note that an industry definition already exists.
