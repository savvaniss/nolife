## Key corrections for the brief

1. Claim 3: US$199/month is the current Full Region tier (since 15 June 2026), not a stale page. Both no-search checkers were wrong on this point.
2. Claim 0/1: the US$78M, US$1.1B, US$1.3B and 21,152 / 6,446 / 139 / 14 figures all come from a December 2024 Linden Lab town hall attributed to Brad Oberwager; US$78M is the 2023 payout.
3. Claim 4: the US$125 Openspace rise was announced and then revised; compliant Openspaces stayed at US$75, Homesteads started at US$95 and were scheduled for US$125 in July 2009.
4. Claim 5: end-2025 and April 2026 region counts could not be corroborated; the cited November 2025 post cannot contain them.

## Additional findings

- Current (2026) land pricing resolved: effective 15 June 2026 LL cut Full Region tier US$209 to US$199/month (30K-LI bonus region US$239 to US$209); Homestead US$109, Openspace and Mainland unchanged; setup remains US$349. The brief's 'two LL pages conflict' is a before/after of this change, not an error. Source: modemworld.me 2026/06/08 relaying LL's announcement.
- Same June 2026 package: minimum LindeX fee for buying L$ falls US$1.49 to US$0.49 (15 June 2026), while Premium and Premium Plus subscription prices rise on new/renewed subscriptions from 8 July 2026 (Premium Plus annual US$249.00 to US$287.88); 'Plus' and 'Premium Plus No Stipend' (introduced Sept 2025) unchanged. This answers the open question on Premium Plus pricing: US$287.88/yr is the post-July-2026 price. Pattern for the successor: LL keeps shifting cost from land to subscriptions, i.e. from builders to consumers.
- The 2024 headline figures (US$1.3B spent, US$1.1B paid out, US$78M paid in 2023, 21,152 earning creators) all come from one December 2024 Linden Lab town hall, under embargo, attributed to Brad Oberwager; Modemworld adds that US$78M was 'up $10 million compared to 2017', i.e. roughly US$68M in 2017 and ~15% nominal growth over six years (flat in real terms).
- Creator concentration is long-standing, not new: in 2009 the top 25 residents earned US$12M, about 22% of the US$55M total (VentureBeat). In 2023, 14 millionaires account for at least 18% of US$78M.
- LL's own economy statistics have been challenged before: Engadget's 2010 review found a typo and a calculation error in the 2009 data and argued the 65% growth claim was unreliable because annual reports and live stat feeds used different definitions. A successor should publish a fixed, documented metric definition and raw data.
- User-side estimate of LL's true take: a 2019 forum calculation quoted by Ryan Schultz put the effective cut at ~31.2% after Marketplace commission, L$ purchase fee and cash-out fee. Supports the design implication of publishing one all-in take; the exact current LindeX sell fee and Tilia payout fee still need confirmation.
- Tilia sale to Thunes (announced 23 Apr 2024) came with an exclusive five-year payments partnership, i.e. LL outsourced its money-transmission rail rather than keep it; a successor can likely license a regulated payout provider from day one rather than build a licensed MSB as LL did in 2019.
- Grid size data points for the long-run series: 29,453 regions Nov 2009 (23,850 private / 5,603 Linden); 25,119 May 2020 (Grid Survey); 27,334 mid-Feb 2022 (secondary). The end-2025 and April 2026 counts in the brief remain uncorroborated after two searches.
- The 2008 Openspace repricing was partly reversed: the final policy kept compliant scenery-only Openspaces at US$75 and created Homesteads at US$95 (then US$125). The trust damage came from the retroactive announcement, not from the eventual price; the brief's 'no grandfathering' point stands for the announcement, not the outcome.
- Method note: page fetching was blocked; all verdicts rest on search-result extracts. 12 of 12 WebSearch calls used; claim 9 was not searched.
