## Source-support issues
- Claims 0 and 1 cite unrealengine.com pages the researcher admits reading only via search excerpts; neither could be fetched here. The substance is corroborated by several independent documents (release-note digests, engine config files such as `BaseScalability.ini` notes).
- Claim 5's URL covers only NVIDIA; AMD/Intel facts come from the separately cited FidelityFX and XeSS release pages (both verified).
- Claim 8's "64 custom materials" is not in the cited DevForum post; it comes from the GamesBeat press briefing.
- Claim 7's Union Avatars closure is single-sourced to a competitor's blog.

## Additional findings relevant to a successor virtual world
- Lumen Lite is **Beta** in UE 5.8, selected via `sg.GlobalIlluminationQuality=1` (`r.Lumen.FinalGatherMethod=0`, irradiance-field gather), about 2x cheaper than Lumen High; SSGI is deprecated in 5.8 and Lumen mesh-SDF detail tracing deprecated since 5.7. The "lite" tier is thus a scalability level inside Lumen, not a separate renderer.
- MegaLights excludes directional (sun) lights by default (`r.MegaLights.DirectionalLights 1` needed) and requires hardware ray tracing; front-layer translucency can double its cost. Important for the Tier A budget.
- Since UE 5.6 Virtual Shadow Maps require Nanite enabled project-wide; VSM on Apple Silicon needs M2+.
- UE 5.8.1 shipped 28 Jul 2026 and is described as the long-lived maintenance baseline; UE6 was announced at State of Unreal (17 Jun 2026) with deep generative-AI integration; Epic also launched "Lore", a free open-source version control system for source and large binary assets (relevant to a UGC asset pipeline).
- Second Life ships mirrors **disabled by default at every graphics preset** (RenderMirrors recommended 0 even at Ultra), so SL's own feature table treats mirrors as opt-in; successor design should assume planar mirrors are a per-object luxury with a hero-probe resolution budget (256 to 2048).
- Roblox Reality's stated unsolved limits: 2K/60 Hz real-time goal, rule enforcement, multiplayer agent simulation, input control; Q2 2026 earnings call framed it as a possible subscription product; early access planned 2027. Roblox also announced web play in Chrome by end of 2026, offline play mid 2027 and "Roblox Everywhere" standalone apps.
- Roblox SLIM (Scalable Lightweight Interactive Models) composites low-poly LODs for streamed-out models and is credited with letting one game run on low-end Android and high-end PC; SLIM for NPCs and welded items is due early 2027. This is the closest shipped analogue to the brief's "auto-generated fallback per asset" recommendation.
- RTXNTC: Cooperative Vector was renamed "LinAlg" in Shader Model 6.10; NTC-on-Load keeps the 2.5 MB disk/PCIe win but still costs 12 MB VRAM (same as BCn); only NTC-on-Sample reduces VRAM.
- The avatar collapse is broader than RPM and Union: Spatial also announced closure (July 2026) per the same vendor reports; VRM (`@pixiv/three-vrm`) is being adopted as the "durable open path" for web avatars because exported files keep working after a hosted service dies.
- AMD FSR 4.1 (SDK v2.2.0, 23 Mar 2026) preceded the RDNA 3 enablement in 4.1.1; Ray Regeneration reached 1.2.0 with optional denoising add-ons in v2.3.0.
