## Additional findings relevant to a successor virtual world

- EU minors guidelines exempt micro and small enterprises; an early-stage successor platform may fall outside them initially but will not once it scales.
- US COPPA Rule amendments (final Jan 2025, effective 23 Jun 2025, compliance deadline 22 Apr 2026) tighten consent for third-party disclosure and data retention; relevant to any under-13 band.
- US app-store age-verification laws (Texas SB 2420 effective 1 Jan 2026, preliminarily enjoined Dec 2025; Utah effective May 2026; Louisiana Jul 2026) push age signals to the OS/app-store layer; a successor should be able to consume app-store age signals rather than run its own ID flow.
- NCMEC total for 2024 is ~20.5m reports (down from 36.2m in 2023 due to report bundling), so year-on-year ratios are not comparable.
- Discord's 2025 breach vector was the human appeals channel at a vendor (5CA), not the age-estimation path; design appeals to avoid raw ID upload.
- The Digital Omnibus on AI proposal may defer the Art 50(2) marking duty for pre-existing generative systems to Feb 2027; check status before scheduling compliance work.
- Impact Theory's total SEC order was over $6.1m, not $500k; the deterrent for "appreciation" marketing of digital items is larger than the brief implies.
