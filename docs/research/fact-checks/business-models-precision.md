## Additional findings relevant to designing a successor world

- Reality Labs' loss history includes 2019 (~$4.5B) and 2020 (~$6.6B); a successor's plan should quote "~$64B 2020-2024, ~$73B through mid-2025" rather than ">$70B since 2020".
- Roblox's "$923M to creators" is an earnings figure (Robux earned at the DevEx rate), not audited cash payouts; the 10-K "developer exchange fees" line is the figure to use in unit-economics tables.
- Roblox regional Robux pricing (2025) and pack-size discounts mean the "30% of face value" shorthand overstates creator share for large packs and understates it for small ones; use Roblox's own "cents per dollar spent" disclosure.
- The Horizon 47.5% take applied to Quest purchases only; web purchases skipped the 30% hardware fee. This matters for the brief's "launch on web/PC and own billing" recommendation, which is directionally supported by Meta's own structure.
- The DappRadar "38 DAU" figure measured on-chain wallet interactions, not avatars present; it is evidence that token-economy metrics diverge from presence metrics, not that only 38 people were in-world.
