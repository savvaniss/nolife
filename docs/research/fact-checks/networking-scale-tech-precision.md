## Additional findings relevant to a successor design

1. The 256 m region width is a compile-time constant in the Second Life client (`REGION_WIDTH_METERS = 256.f` in `indra_constants.h`) and is baked into SLURL math, world-map mip levels and wind/terrain code. Changing it means changing the client protocol, which is why Linden Lab never did. A successor should never expose a spatial partition size to the client.
2. Star Citizen is now past "static": Alpha 4.10 (LIVE by Oct 2026) adds quasi-dynamic meshing, i.e. instanced sub-areas on separate game servers with an instance manager, while the shard itself stays at ~500 players. That is close to the brief's own "cells + event mode" recommendation and is the most recent shipped reference.
3. Roblox FY2025 10-K: cost of revenue +34% (payment processing) and developer exchange fees +63% (US$580.3M more) grew faster than bookings; infrastructure and T&S (+26%) is now the third-largest cost line behind DevEx and cost of revenue. Creator payouts, not servers, are the fastest-growing cost of a UGC world.
4. VRChat's three successive records in Q1 2026 (148,886 -> 156,716 -> 158,192) were all driven by Japanese virtual concerts in instanced worlds, reinforcing the brief's "many small instances plus events" pattern; VRChat also launched full iOS/Android apps on 24 Oct 2025.
5. For GPU-streaming cost models use US$0.526/h (Linux, us-east-1) or ~US$0.16/h Spot for a T4 g4dn.xlarge; a Windows build adds ~US$0.18/h in licence cost. The per-user-hour gap to a native client is still two orders of magnitude.
