## Additional findings (new facts that matter for a successor world)

- **Switch 2 figure confirmed**: 23.68M cumulative at 30 Jun 2026; 3.82M in the April-June quarter (-34.4% YoY vs the 5.82M launch quarter); FY to 31 Mar 2027 forecast held at 16.5M; quarterly revenue ¥517.8B, operating profit ¥142.5B; 9.46M Switch 2 games sold in the quarter, Mario Kart World 15.39M lifetime (iPhoneInCanada, GodIsAGeek, wnhub).
- **IDC VR numbers (via The Register, 2 Jan 2026)**: Apple shipped no Vision Pro in Q1-Q3 2025 after halting manufacturing, relaunched with M5 for Q4 and shipped ~45,000 in 2025 (vs 390,000 in 2024); Meta shipped 1.7M Quest in Q1-Q3 2025, down 16% YoY (a nine-month figure, not full year; 2024 base 5.6M); IDC expected the whole MR/VR headset market to fall 42.8% in 2025 (earlier forecast was -12%). Tech-insider's "-42.3% Quest" and "4,500 Vision Pro in Q4" figures conflict and are likely the market-wide decline and a quarterly number respectively. For design: the VR installed base shrank in 2025; flat-screen-first remains the right default.
- **Lumen Lite is a preset, not a separate renderer**: outlets call it "Lumen Lite (Lumen Medium)", irradiance fields with probe occlusion, up to 2x faster than the Lumen High preset; Epic maps scalability presets to frame-rate targets (Medium = 60 fps on Switch 2 and mid-spec PC). Mesh Terrain and the Procedural Vegetation Editor are experimental in 5.8. A Brazilian outlet puts UE6 in 2027 (secondary).
- **FSR 4.1 on RDNA 3 is an INT8 port**: RX 7000 only, RDNA 3 APUs/iGPUs excluded (AMD building a lighter model), 300+ games; enabled by Adrenalin 26.6.2. Handheld/APU devices (Steam Deck, Z-series handhelds) therefore still lack ML upscaling from AMD, which matters for a Tier B handheld target.
- **DLSS 5x/6x is still gated**: 6x is officially "in development"; end users initially reach it only via NVIDIA App DLSS Overrides; NVIDIA claims up to +35% fps from 4x to 6x in 4K path-traced titles; latency is the acknowledged cost. Dynamic Multi Frame Generation ships in the same DLSS 4.5 Streamline SDK.
- **Steam survey caveats**: the RTX 5070 is a 12 GB card; "Other" (8.82%) is larger than any named card; the +2.30 pt monthly jump is the largest single-month move in Valve's table; opt-in sampling noise. 32 GB at 42.22% is the largest share, not a majority.
- **Roblox platform direction at RDC26 beyond rendering**: browser play in Chrome with no install by end of 2026; offline solo mode by mid-2027; "Roblox Everywhere" standalone app distribution on mobile/PC/consoles "soon"; Highly Engaged Player monetization threshold lowered from 250 to 100 in November 2026; "Roblox Reality" Early Access 2027. A zero-install browser entry point is now also Roblox's stated plan, which reinforces the WebGPU/WebGL2 entry-tier recommendation.
- **Avatar vendor collapse detail**: Netflix took ~20 RPM staff and the CTO only; Netflix has "no timeline" for using the avatars in its games, so the technology is not re-emerging as a licensable service. Union Avatars had no acquirer and no successor product. Nearly all post-mortem coverage comes from competitors (Avatar SDK, Genies), so treat their "who's alive" roundups as marketing.
- **Second Life rollout mechanics**: the server-side flags for mirrors, PBR terrain and 2K textures shipped in a simulator release dated 2024-04-13, roughly two months before the viewer went to release; 2K uploads carry a higher upload fee. The gating pattern (server feature flag + viewer capability + upload price) is a reusable budgeting lever.
