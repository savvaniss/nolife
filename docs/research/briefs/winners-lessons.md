# What the winners did right: Roblox, Fortnite/UEFN, Minecraft, GTA RP, FFXIV, Habbo, Zepeto, Discord

**Prepared for:** the "nolife" successor-to-Second-Life design brief. **Date:** 2026-10-09.

## Method note

This is the live-search rewrite of the earlier prior-knowledge draft (`winners-lessons.prior.md`). I used all **20 WebSearch calls** in the shared budget (standard mode throughout). Page fetching (WebFetch/curl) is blocked in this sandbox, so every citation rests on the quoted extracts the search tool returned, not on reading the pages themselves; where an extract summarised a secondary site rather than a primary document I say so. Facts I could not search are kept only where load-bearing and marked **(prior knowledge, unsearched)**. Figures that the search contradicted or could not confirm are flagged explicitly, including several numbers from the prior draft that turned out to be wrong (Grow a Garden's record, the FFXIV 30M figure's composition, the Fortnite 2024 payout, Zepeto's "400M").

**Verified live:** Roblox Q2 2026 and FY2025 results, DevEx totals and rate changes, Roblox concurrency records; Fortnite Creator Economy 2.0 structure, cumulative $1B payout, in-island purchases, concert records; Rockstar/Cfx.re date and policy outline; FiveM Steam peaks and GTA VI date; NoPixel 4.0 date and comparable RP server pricing; FFXIV 30M milestone, lottery and demolition rules; Habbo Origins launch and 2021 MAU; Zepeto 2022 figures; Discord MAU, IPO filing, Social SDK and age-assurance timeline.
**Not verified live:** Minecraft 2025 sales/MAU (only aggregator estimates surfaced), Minecraft Marketplace cumulative payouts, NoPixel's own price list, Zepeto 2024-2026 status, Habbo 2025 financials, Fortnite OG daily record, Fortnite 2025 annual payout.

## Findings

### 1. Roblox: the scale benchmark, now past its peak-growth moment

- **Q2 2026 (quarter ended June 30, 2026, reported July 30):** 123M DAU (+10% YoY), 29B hours engaged (+5%), revenue ~$1.5B (+36%), bookings $1.6B (+8%), net loss $185M, average bookings per DAU $12.66 (-2%), 27M monthly unique payers (+15%), free cash flow $294M. Management guided to YoY declines in bookings and FCF for Q3 2026 and attributed the bookings miss to engagement shifting from high-monetising 2025 viral games to lower-monetising evergreen titles, plus a recommendation-algorithm change favouring retention. (Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001315098/000162828026051059/ex991-robloxq22026earnin.htm ; https://www.shacknews.com/article/150208/roblox-daus-grew-10-percent-yoy)
- **The 18+ cohort is the growth engine:** DAU and hours among users 18+ grew 32% and 27% YoY in Q2 2026; Japan (+67%) and India (+64%) led regional growth. (Source: https://respawn.outlookindia.com/gaming/gaming-news/roblox-q2-2026-revenue-soars-36-to-15b-despite-bookings-miss)
- **Q3 2025 was the high-water mark** at roughly 152M DAU; Q2 2026 DAU is down from that level, i.e. the viral-game wave of 2025 did not fully stick. (Source: https://www.shacknews.com/article/150208/roblox-daus-grew-10-percent-yoy)
- **FY2025:** revenue $4.9B (+36%); Q4 2025 bookings $2.2B (+63%). **DevEx payouts exceeded $1.5B in 2025**, versus $923M in 2024; Q4 2025 alone was $477M (+70% YoY, ~33.7% of revenue). The top 1,000 creators averaged ~$1.3M each in 2025 (+50%). Earnings call Feb 5, 2026. (Source: https://quartr.com/events/roblox-rblx-q4-2025_ozGB34Pf ; https://www.aol.com/articles/roblox-rblx-q4-2025-earnings-224200551.html ; https://wnhub.io/news/finance/item-50058)
- **DevEx rate:** rose 8.5% from $0.0035 to **$0.0038 per Robux on Sept 5, 2025** (100,000 Robux = $380); 30,000 earned-Robux minimum, Premium and age 13+ required (eligibility per a third-party calculator). In April 2026 Roblox announced a **42% DevEx rate increase for eligible spend by age-checked US players 18+** (game passes, subscriptions, private servers), effective June 8, 2026. (Source: https://fintool.com/app/research/companies/RBLX/earnings/Q4%202025 ; https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/04/roblox-fuels-high-fidelity-games-over-18-players-increases-qualifying-devex-rate-42)
- **Concurrency records (corrects the prior draft):** Grow a Garden peaked at ~22.3M concurrent and Steal a Brainrot at ~20.1M during the joint "Admin War" event of **Aug 23, 2025**, pushing platform-wide concurrency to **~47.3M**, above Steam's record. Steal a Brainrot then hit **25.4M on Oct 7, 2025**, the first game past 25M. The prior draft's "21M on June 21, 2025" did not appear in any extract. (Source: https://www.pocketgamer.biz/robloxs-steal-a-brainrot-becomes-first-game-to-surpass-25m-concurrent-players ; https://www.tubefilter.com/?p=188004)

### 2. Fortnite / UEFN: the engagement pool, now hybridised with direct sales

- **Creator Economy 2.0 (March 2023)** distributes **40% of net revenue from the Item Shop and most real-money purchases** (plus 50% of Sponsored Row revenue) to island creators proportionally by engagement. (Source: https://www.fortnite.com/news/introducing-the-creator-economy-2-0 ; https://shacknews.com/article/134711/fortnite-creator-economy-40-percent-revenue)
- **2024 payouts:** sources disagree, $352M (with ~70,000 active creators) versus $320M; the prior draft's "$320M in 2023" is likewise unconfirmed. (Source: https://everybody.beehiiv.com/p/the-business-of-fortnite ; https://esports.gg/guides/fortnite/how-do-fortnite-creators-make-money/)
- **Cumulative $1B paid to UEFN creators** was crossed in January 2026 and announced at State of Unreal, Unreal Fest Chicago, **June 17, 2026**. (Source: https://gamesbeat.com/epic-games-has-paid-out-1-billion-to-creators-since-launching-unreal-editor-for-fortnite-in-2023/ ; https://tech-insider.org/ca/fortnite-creator-economy-1-billion-2026/)
- **Direct sales arrived:** island-specific purchasable items from Sept 2025; a 75% bonus share for creators who bring new or 6-month-lapsed players from Nov 2025; **in-island transactions from January 2026**. By June 2026 Epic said **3,000+ maps earn more from in-game transactions than from the engagement pool**, and **47% of all player hours are in creator islands, up from 35% in May 2025**. The exact creator split on in-island sales was not in any extract. (Source: https://profitable.app/tools/fortnite-creator-payout-calculator ; https://respawn.outlookindia.com/gaming/gaming-news/epics-uefn-payouts-reached-1-billion-since-launch)
- **Events as the shared "when":** Marshmello (Feb 2019) 10.7M concurrent; Travis Scott "Astronomical" (April 2020) 12.3M; the Galactus event (Dec 2020) 15.3M, still the largest in-game event; **"Remix: The Finale" 14M+ concurrent plus ~3M stream viewers**, Epic's all-time concert record (fortnite.gg tracked ~14.3M). One extract dated Remix as Nov 30, 2025; the prior draft and the underlying coverage date it Nov 30, 2024, so treat the year as needing a primary check. The "Fortnite OG 44.7M in a day" figure did not surface **(prior knowledge, unsearched)**. (Source: https://musicbusinessworldwide.com/fortnites-remix-the-finale-concert-draws-over-14m-players-breaking-records ; https://tubefilter.com/2020/04/24/travis-scott-fortnite-concert-all-time-record/ ; https://www.pocketgamer.biz/fortnites-dj-marshmello-concert-surpasses-games-concurrent-record-with-107-players)

### 3. Minecraft: ownership and a self-hosted server economy (weakly verified)

- The only official sales figure that surfaced is **300M copies at Minecraft Live, Oct 2023**; aggregators estimate 325M-400M by 2025 and ~20-25M new copies a year. MAU estimates (aggregators, not Microsoft) put a **peak of ~222.5M in June 2025** after *A Minecraft Movie* (April 2025) and ~212M in 2026. Treat all 2025-2026 figures as estimates. (Source: https://sqmagazine.co.uk/minecraft-statistics/ ; https://hosthavoc.com/blog/minecraft-statistics)
- **Marketplace economics:** at launch (June 2017) creators earned $1M in the first three months across 12 partners; Mojang described the creator share as a "majority" of what remains after the 30% app-store cut. The widely repeated "$500M cumulative" and "~70% creator share" claims come from aggregators with no Mojang statement found. Marketplace Pass is a monthly subscription to 150+ items; its price and launch date were not in extracts **(prior knowledge: $3.99/month, 2024, unsearched)**. (Source: https://fortune.com/2017/09/05/minecraft-marketplace-microsoft-million ; https://www.demandsage.com/?p=16879)
- Java-server monetisation via the 2014 EULA (cosmetics and ranks allowed, pay-to-win banned), Tebex as the dominant store, and Hypixel's ~216k concurrent record are **(prior knowledge, unsearched)**.

### 4. GTA RP / FiveM / NoPixel: roleplay as the purpose engine, now first-party

- **Rockstar announced on Aug 11, 2023 that Cfx.re (FiveM, RedM) had joined Rockstar**, eight years after Rockstar banned the team in 2015 as an "unauthorized alternate multiplayer service". Terms were undisclosed; Cfx.re said day-to-day operations would not change. (Source: https://gameworldobserver.com/2023/08/11/cfx-re-joins-rockstar-deal-fivem-redm-roleplay ; https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/rockstar-acquires-gta-5-roleplay-devs-cfx-re/)
- **Monetisation rules (secondary sources):** Tebex is the exclusive authorised payment partner; servers may sell cosmetics and queue priority but not in-game currency or items that confer gameplay advantage; real-world brands, music and vehicles are banned without licences. The governing Creator Platform License Agreement was rewritten **Jan 12, 2026**, and Rockstar's RP-server policy was last updated Jan 7, 2025. (Source: https://www.gameserverkings.com/knowledge-base/fivem/server-monetisation/)
- **Scale:** FiveM was integrated into Steam in January 2026 and immediately out-peaked GTA V itself; SteamDB/Steam Charts show an **all-time Steam peak of 221,062 (May 2026)**, ~203k in June 2026. This counts only Steam-launched players. (Source: https://steamcharts.com/app/2676230 ; https://players.com.ua/en/news/fivem-surpasses-gta-5-in-player-count-over-the-past-week/)
- **NoPixel 4.0 launched Dec 15, 2023** (the prior draft said spring 2024). NoPixel's own priority-queue price list did not surface; comparable whitelisted servers charge **$25/$50/$100/$200 per month** (Rikers: Bronze to Diamond) or $8.99-$14.99 (Enigma), all via Tebex, all explicitly "subject to server capacity". (Source: https://rockstarintel.com/gta-rp-server-nopixel-4-update-release-date/ ; https://ascenddev.tebex.io/category/priority-roles ; https://enigmashop.tebex.io/category/enigma-queue-priorit)
- **GTA VI ships Nov 19, 2026** on PS5/Xbox Series (delayed from fall 2025, then May 26, 2026). Whether it carries RP tooling at launch is speculation. (Source: https://www.guru3d.com/story/rockstar-delays-gta-vi-again-new-release-date-set-for-november-2026/)

### 5. FFXIV: engineered scarcity and the venue scene

- **30M+ registered players**, announced at Fan Festival 2024 Tokyo (January 2024); the count includes free-trial accounts. Dawntrail (July 2024) produced the highest concurrency since A Realm Reborn (2013). Yoshida called FFXIV the most profitable Final Fantasy ever in 2022. Subscription $12.99 (Entry) / $14.99-15.99 (Standard) per a third-party aggregator. (Source: https://gamesmea.com/final-fantasy-xiv-online-now-has-over-30-million-players ; https://gameworldobserver.com/2024/07/05/final-fantasy-14-record-concurrent-players-dawntrail ; https://www.nme.com/news/final-fantasy-xiv-is-the-most-profitable-game-in-the-series-3069506 ; https://subger.com/en/service/ffxiv-sub)
- **Housing lottery (patch 6.1, first window April 12, 2022):** five-day entry, full plot price as the ticket, one plot per period, four days to confirm. **Auto-demolition at 45 days** without entering the house (login is not enough), with warnings from day 30 and an 80% gil refund; the timer was suspended during the pandemic and the Ukraine war, re-suspended on NA data centres in early 2025 and **resumed June 6, 2025**. One guide claims 30 days; the patch-note-based sources say 45. (Source: https://www.inverse.com/gaming/final-fantasy-14-housing-updates-lottery-empyreum-patch-6-1 ; https://techraptor.net/gaming/news/ffxiv-house-demolition-suspension-reinstated-temporarily ; https://expertbeacon.com/how-long-does-it-take-to-lose-your-house-in-ff14/)
- The venue scene (clubs and cafés in player housing, promoted on Discord and FFXIV Venues, grey-zone client mods such as Mare Synchronos) and Island Sanctuary as abundant private space are **(prior knowledge, unsearched)**.

### 6. Habbo: 25 years of pixel persistence

- **Habbo Hotel: Origins launched June 18, 2024**, a six-month restoration of the 2005 client from "an old decrepit server with some long-lost files"; 18+, no casinos or gambling, "community-led" with an in-world voting forum; a Steam release was announced later. (Source: https://80.lv/articles/the-original-2005-habbo-hotel-opens-its-doors-later-today ; https://ggrecon.com/articles/habbo-hotel-fans-have-a-major-issue-with-the-games-return)
- Azerion took a controlling stake in 2018 and **fully acquired Sulake in January 2021**, when Habbo had **800k+ MAU across 115 countries** and sister app Hotel Hideaway 840k MAU; a later figure was 850k. No 2025 MAU or revenue figures surfaced. (Source: https://www.pocketgamer.biz/azerion-fully-acquires-habbo-hotel-dev-sulake ; https://gamesbeat.com/sulakes-habbo-dips-its-toe-into-nfts-with-donation-to-player-community/)

### 7. Zepeto: avatar-first and mobile, but the public numbers stop in 2022

- Naver Z reported **300M registered users and ~20M MAU in March 2022**, 95% outside Korea; later 2022 reports cite 340M registered. The "400M" figure in the brief did not appear in any extract. (Source: https://m.kedglobal.com/newsView/ked202203040009 ; https://tynmagazine.com/asias-largest-metaverse-platform-zepeto-ramps-up-global-expansion/)
- **Creator density:** 2M creators and 68M creator items sold (March 2022), 2.83M creators by Sept 2022 with top creators reporting ~15M KRW a month; later claims of 5B creations and 3B items sold. (Source: https://view.asiae.co.kr/en/article/2022091915001729836 ; https://tynmagazine.com/asias-largest-metaverse-platform-zepeto-ramps-up-global-expansion/)
- **Post-pandemic fragility:** Korean weekly users fell to 133,708 in May 2022, the lowest since mid-2021, as teenagers returned to school. The platform was still running events in January 2026; 2024-2026 restructuring and MAU are unverified. (Source: https://asianews.network/number-of-metaverse-users-hits-lowest-point-after-social-distancing-rules-lifted)

### 8. Discord: the social layer every world rents

- **200M+ MAU** (company figure, April 2025); third-party estimates ~$725M revenue in 2024, mostly Nitro; last private valuation ~$15B (2021), secondary-market ~$8.5B (June 2026). **Confidential IPO filing in January 2026** with Goldman Sachs and JPMorgan; no public S-1 yet. (Source: https://fintool.com/news/discord-ipo-confidential-filing ; https://techstackipo.com/ipo/discord)
- **Platform APIs:** Embedded App SDK / Activities (iframe web apps in calls) first appeared in 2024; the **Social SDK launched Aug 13, 2025** for in-game voice, messaging and friend graphs (announced at GDC March 2025, prior knowledge), extended with native mobile linking in March 2026. (Source: https://gamefromscratch.com/discord-launch-game-development-sdk/ ; https://www.pocketgamer.biz/industry/discord/)
- **Age assurance:** teen-by-default and age-assurance changes roll out globally in H2 2026; Discord says >90% of users will never be asked to verify, and tells developers no SDK or integration changes are needed. (Source: https://support-dev.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/38338398970263-Upcoming-Discord-Age-Assurance-Changes-What-You-Need-To-Know)

## Patterns / root causes

1. **Purpose is supplied by loops, roles or scarcity, never by tools alone.** Roblox and Fortnite deliver goals through games; GTA RP through jobs and factions on a 200-slot server people pay to queue for; FFXIV through a plot you can lose in 45 days; Zepeto and Habbo through appearance status. Second Life shipped tools and land and left purpose to the user.
2. **Growth is now coming from adults, and the platforms are re-pricing for them.** Roblox's 18+ DAU grew 32% YoY in Q2 2026, and its 42% DevEx bonus applies only to age-checked 18+ US spend; Habbo Origins is 18+; Discord's assurance rollout is explicitly teen-by-default. The "serious adult world" gap Second Life once filled is being approached from below by the youth platforms.
3. **Engagement pools and direct sales are converging.** Epic started with a pure 40% engagement pool and added direct item sales (2025-2026); 3,000+ islands now earn more from sales than from the pool. Roblox started with direct sales and added engagement-weighted rate bonuses. Neither model alone retains creators.
4. **The platform take is high but tolerated because distribution is free and discovery works.** Roblox creators get ~$0.0038 per Robux that cost the buyer ~$0.0125 **(prior knowledge, unsearched)**; Epic pools 40% of net; Minecraft Marketplace pays a "majority" after the 30% store cut; FiveM servers keep Tebex-net revenue but under Rockstar's rules. Revolts happen when the formula is opaque, not when it is low.
5. **Virality is not retention.** Roblox's DAU fell from ~152M (Q3 2025) to 123M (Q2 2026) and per-hour monetisation dropped as play shifted from viral 2025 titles to evergreen games. Zepeto's weekly users collapsed when schools reopened. Persistent worlds need the evergreen loop, not the spike.
6. **Events create the shared "when".** 10.7M, 12.3M, 14M+ concurrent for concerts; 15.3M for a narrative event; 47M across Roblox during a two-game crossover. Second Life has no calendar that 10% of its users share.
7. **Scarce shared land, abundant private land.** FFXIV's lottery, full-price ticket and demolition timer make a house a status object; Roblox and Fortnite have no land and lose nothing. Second Life's unlimited mainland made land a cost.
8. **Everyone rents the social graph from Discord**, which is pre-IPO and rolling out age assurance; Discord says integrations are unaffected, but the dependency is total (whitelists, schedules, moderation, payments links).
9. **Legitimisation beats litigation.** Rockstar banned FiveM in 2015 and hired it in 2023; Hytale, Mare Synchronos-style mods and Marketplace partners show the pattern: the platform that absorbs its modders keeps them.

## Design implications for nolife

1. **Ship roles and loops in the first five minutes:** job boards, crews, venue shifts and build commissions modelled on GTA RP whitelisting and Roblox progression, not an empty orientation island.
2. **Lead with the 18+ market the winners are only now discovering:** verified-adult identity tiers, adult districts, real-money cash-out and professional use, pricing the DevEx-style "adult spend bonus" from day one instead of retrofitting it.
3. **Hybrid creator economics with a published split:** an engagement pool of 40-50% of net revenue distributed by retention and new-player acquisition, plus direct sales at a stated creator share (target 70%+), with a 75%-style bounty for creators who bring new or lapsed users.
4. **Scarce public land, unlimited private land:** lottery allocation with full-price tickets, a 45-day use-it-or-lose-it rule with refunds, and instanced private "sanctuaries" for building; publish the ward map so scarcity is legible.
5. **A first-party event calendar engineered for 100k+ synchronised concurrency**, with creator-run events (venues, DJs) scheduled in-world rather than on Discord.
6. **Own voice, text, crews, scheduling and whitelists natively**, interoperate through the Discord Social SDK as a bridge, and plan for Discord's age-assurance and IPO-era policy drift.
7. **Avatar identity continuity as the differentiator:** one persistent self with portable inventory and reputation across shards, which none of Roblox, Fortnite, FFXIV or Discord offers.
8. **Adopt Rockstar's RP monetisation rules wholesale:** subscriptions, cosmetics, priority access and creator sales allowed; loot boxes, pay-to-win and unlicensed brands banned; one authorised payment rail.
9. **Instant, mobile and free onboarding** with one-tap account creation and a streamed client, because every platform that scaled is free-to-start and mobile-reachable **(prior knowledge for mobile share figures, unsearched)**.
