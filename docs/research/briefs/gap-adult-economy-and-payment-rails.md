# Gap research: Second Life's adult economy and whether a licensed cash-out rail will carry it

Date: 2026-10-09. Author: research subagent (nolife postmortem).

## Method note

14 WebSearch calls were used (the hard cap). Page fetching (WebFetch/curl) is blocked in this sandbox, so every figure below comes from the quoted extracts that the search tool returned, not from reading the pages. Where a claim comes from memory rather than a search hit it is marked **[prior knowledge, unverified]**. The two biggest evidence gaps are (a) no Linden Lab statement on the UK Online Safety Act was found at all, and (b) no current Marketplace maturity-filter listing counts were found; both are reported as absences, not guessed.

## Findings

### 1. How much of Second Life is adult-rated?

**Land.** The only hard share figure that surfaced is from the November 2009 Adult-content migration: 1,325 private regions and 348 mainland regions (mostly Zindra) were rated Adult, which Linden Lab put at **5.69% of the 29,409-region grid** (Source: https://wiki.secondlife.com/wiki/Adult_content). Zindra launched with a 157-region map, at least 531 region names were reserved, and the wiki still describes it only as "hundreds of regions" (Source: https://wiki.secondlife.com:443/wiki/Zindra). In 2026 Linden Lab added **92 more regions to Zindra**, released by entry draw rather than auction (Source: https://modemworld.me/tag/mainland/), and in March 2026 it "quietly opened" an official adult hub (Source: https://www.hypergridbusiness.com/2026/03/linden-lab-quietly-opens-adult-hub-in-second-life/). Both are evidence that the Lab is still investing in adult land in 2026, not retreating from it. Note that the 5.69% counts only regions whose *owners* chose the Adult flag; a large share of sexual roleplay in SL happens on Moderate-rated private estates where nudity is allowed, so the Adult-flag share understates the adult economy. Wagner James Au's 2008 book reported about **18% of land dedicated to "mature activity" and 13.6% of residents saying they engage in virtual sex regularly** (Source: https://venturebeat.com/technology/book-review-the-making-of-second-life). No 2020s traffic split by maturity rating was found.

**Marketplace.** No source gives a count or percentage of Adult-rated Marketplace listings; the Marketplace adopted the General/Moderate/Adult tiers in January 2011 and Adult results require an opt-in (Source: https://community.secondlife.com/blogs/entry/1-maturity-changes-in-the-marketplace/). From August 17, 2026 the Lab auto-unlists products whose merchant has not logged in for a year and that have not sold in a year, so any future count will shrink (Source: https://modemworld.me/2026/08/12/linden-lab-announces-new-second-life-marketplace-maintenance-policies/). **[prior knowledge, unverified]**: mesh bodies (Maitreya, Legacy, eBody), genitals/"bits" add-ons, animation engines (AVsitter, Lovense-style HUDs) and furniture with sex animations are among the highest-grossing Marketplace categories and most are listed as Moderate, not Adult, which is why a maturity-filter count would understate the intimacy-oriented economy even if it existed.

**Revenue.** No Lab or blogger estimate of the adult share of L$ flow or cash-out was found. Company-reported economic activity was about $1.5M/day in 2007 (Source: https://venturebeat.com/technology/book-review-the-making-of-second-life). **[prior knowledge, unverified]**: Linden Lab last published ~US$650M/year in user-to-user transactions and ~US$80M/year cashed out by creators (2015–2019 era); no adult breakdown was ever released.

**Bottom line for (1):** the honest answer is that the adult share is unmeasured in public data. The best available anchors are 5.69% of regions Adult-flagged (2009), 18% of land "mature" (2007/8), 13.6% of residents regularly having virtual sex (survey), and continued 2026 Zindra expansion.

### 2. Linden Lab and the UK Online Safety Act

Ofcom's "highly effective age assurance" duty for services showing pornographic content took effect **25 July 2025**; acceptable methods are open banking, photo-ID matching, facial age estimation, mobile-operator checks, digital ID, credit-card checks and email-based estimation, and self-declaration is not enough (Source: https://www.kingsleynapley.co.uk/our-insights/articles/new-child-safety-duties-under-the-online-safety-act-what-online-platforms-must-know/).

**No Linden Lab statement about the OSA was found in any search**, including an extended-mode search. What was found: a Second Life forum thread titled "New UK censorship requirements" where residents discussed Ofcom's draft guidance and speculated that requiring a UK credit card might pass (Source: https://community.secondlife.com/forums/topic/518772-new-uk-censorship-requirements/); a 2026 forum thread "Age Verification in 2026" in which residents state that "there is no official age verification in SL. If you are 18 in RL and use your actual birthday, that's all the verification you need right now" (Source: https://community.secondlife.com/forums/topic/533810-age-verification-in-2026/); and the March 2026 Hypergrid Business report on the new adult hub, which says "Second Life has no real age verification system, however, meaning underage players could technically still access the hub" (Source: https://www.hypergridbusiness.com/2026/03/linden-lab-quietly-opens-adult-hub-in-second-life/).

So as of mid-2026, **SL's Adult gate is still self-declared date of birth at signup**, the model adopted in July 2012 when the Lab dropped the Aristotle/Integrity document check (Source: https://modemworld.me/2012/07/10/ll-revises-sl-age-verification/). Region owners can additionally require "payment info on file" (a card or PayPal purchase), which the Lab itself used as an Adult-access proxy in 2009 (Source: https://community.secondlife.com/knowledgebase/english/accessing-adult-land-and-content-r236/). The Terms of Service still say residents "will submit to account verification as required by Linden" as a condition of Adult access (Source: https://lindenlab.com/legal/second-life-terms-and-conditions). Ofcom has fined at least one small adult service £50,000 for skipping checks (Source: https://www.theregister.com/2025/11/21/ofcom_osa_fine_undress/). Contrast VRChat, which built Persona-based 18+ verification (rolled out to VRC+ subscribers 28 January 2025), announced UK-first "content gating" that forces the Sexually Suggestive filter on for non-assured users, and in June 2026 said it will shift toward age-estimation providers (Source: https://wiki.vrchat.com/wiki/Age_Assurance; https://hello.vrchat.com/blog/june-2026-safety-update). VRChat explicitly does not permit pornographic content even in 18+ instances (Source: https://wiki.vrchat.com/wiki/Age_Verification).

### 3. The 2025 payment-processor campaign and its reach into virtual worlds

Collective Shout sent 1,067 emails to Visa, Mastercard, PayPal and others after Steam ignored complaints about *No Mercy* (Source: https://stevivor.com/news/aussie-group-collective-shout-claims-responsibility-for-removal-of-nsfw-games-on-steam-itch-io). Valve removed titles in July 2025 and said the pressure came through its processors and acquiring banks, who cited **Mastercard Rule 5.12.7** (brand-damaging transactions, including legal content that is "clearly offensive and lack[s] serious artistic value") (Source: https://www.gamingonlinux.com/2025/08/mastercard-release-a-statement-about-game-stores-payment-processors-and-adult-content/comment_id=281746; https://www.toy-people.com/en/?p=102325). Mastercard denied evaluating any game (Source: https://techcrunch.com/2025/08/03/mastercard-denies-pressuring-game-platforms-valve-tells-a-different-story). itch.io deindexed all NSFW content on 24 July 2025 to protect its card processing, later re-indexing free adult content (Source: https://www.thesixthaxis.com/2025/07/24/itch-io-delists-all-adult-nsfw-content-due-to-pressure-from-payment-processors-and-collective-shout/; https://wbgsv0b.gigazine.net/gsc_news/en/20250805-mastercard-not-required-restrictions-steam-claims-otherwise). Japanese storefront DLsite lost Visa/Mastercard acceptance entirely (Source: https://automaton-media.com/en/news/dlsite-disables-visa-mastercard-payment-after-attempt-to-outsmart-credit-card-companies/). In October 2025 the UK government said it would not intervene to make the schemes process adult transactions (Source: https://www.thepinknews.com/2025/10/08/visa-and-mastercard-censorship-uk-government).

**No search result showed Second Life's Marketplace, Tilia/Thunes, or VRChat being targeted** by this wave. That is a meaningful negative: SL is the largest adult-inclusive UGC economy taking Visa/Mastercard, and it was not named. **[prior knowledge, unverified]**: the plausible reason is that SL's L$ purchase is coded as a general virtual-currency purchase, not an adult MCC, and the sexual content is in-world rather than on the checkout page the acquirer sees.

**2026 scheme rules.** Under the Visa Integrity Risk Program, adult content is Tier 1 under MCC 5967 and a February 2026 update requires that MCC for all adult content including AI-generated material, with miscoding treated as an enforcement matter; VIRP expects age verification, content moderation and real-time transaction monitoring (Source: https://www.legitscript.com/regulatory-and-card-brand-compliance/adult-escort-and-dating-merchants-are-your-practices-in-line-with-virp-updates/; https://paymentcloudinc.com/blog/visa-integrity-risk-program-high-risk-merchants-guide/). For UGC sites Visa expects uploader verification before posting and documented consent of everyone depicted (Source: https://segpay.com/blog/visa-integrity-risk-program-high-risk-merchants/). Mastercard's AN 5196 requires age/identity verification of uploaders and all persons depicted plus pre-publication review (Source: https://verifymy.io/resources/regulation/card-brand-requirements). A secondary source says Mastercard's Specialty Merchant Registration overhaul effective 1 May 2026 doubled the annual registration fee to about $1,000 and added ~$0.02/transaction and a 0.10% assessment (Source: https://beancount.io/blog/2026/08/13/mastercard-specialty-merchant-registration-overhaul-2026-guide) — unverified against the bulletin.

### 4. Do licensed rails accept adult-inclusive UGC economies?

- **Stripe**: adult content is on the *prohibited* list (not merely restricted), including escorts, pay-per-view, fetish services, adult live chat and AI-generated adult content; the list was updated in May 2026 (Source: https://conductatlas.com/platform/stripe/stripe-restricted-businesses-list/adult-content-prohibition-and-restriction/; https://terms.law/Privacy-Watchdog/payment-processors/stripe/). A UGC world running on Stripe would be in breach the moment it hosted a sex club.
- **Adyen**: "adult goods" are *restricted* (possible with approval) while fetish products, sexual/offensive content, strip clubs and escort agencies are *prohibited*; separate columns apply to merchants vs platform/partner businesses, and local payment methods can be stricter (Source: https://adyen.com/legal/list-restricted-prohibited).
- **Tilia/Thunes**: Thunes agreed to buy Tilia in April 2024 with an exclusive 5-year processing/payout deal for Linden Lab; Tilia handles card and PayPal purchases of L$ and bank cash-outs, separately from the LindeX (Source: https://modemworld.me/2024/04/23/linden-lab-to-sell-payments-platform-tilia-to-thunes/). No published Tilia or Thunes adult-content policy was found. **[prior knowledge, unverified]**: Tilia holds US state money-transmitter licences; SL's rail works because the licensed entity settles a *currency* (L$) under KYC'd accounts and the acquirer's merchant is "virtual currency for a virtual world", not an adult merchant.
- Monitoring obligations in practice are set by the scheme programs above (VIRP, AN 5196), not by the processors' marketing pages: uploader KYC, consent records, pre-publication review, complaint handling, and transaction monitoring, plus MCC 5967 coding, which triggers higher fees and Tier-1 assessment.

### 5. Design precedents and their cost in users

- **Full exit from adult content (Tumblr, Dec 2018)**: Similarweb traffic fell ~29–30% within three months (151M fewer page views Dec–Feb) and the site sold to Automattic for about $3M in 2019 (Source: https://thenextweb.com/insider/2019/03/14/tumblr-traffic-dropped-by-nearly-100m-the-month-after-it-banned-porn/; https://thepinknews.com/2019/05/03/tumblr-may-be-sold-to-pornhub-after-porn-ban-leads-to-huge-traffic-drop).
- **Announce-then-reverse (OnlyFans, Aug 2021)**: ban attributed to "banking partners and payout providers", reversed within a week after agreeing to the schemes' stricter adult policies (Source: https://www.cbc.ca/lite/story/1.6148524). No user-loss figure published.
- **Deindex, keep selling (itch.io, Jul 2025)**: adult pages hidden from browse/search, not deleted; partial restoration for free content (Source: https://www.gosugamers.net/entertainment/news/76389-itch-io-follows-steam-in-removing-adult-games-amid-pressure-from-payment-processors).
- **Verified-adult sub-tier without porn (VRChat, 2025–26)**: Persona verification unlocks 18+ instances and will gate suggestive content for unverified UK users, but pornography stays banned platform-wide (Source: https://wiki.vrchat.com/wiki/Age_Assurance).
- **Payment-info-on-file as age proxy (Second Life, 2009–present)**: estate owners can restrict to residents with payment info on file; the Lab itself used this as the Adult gate in 2009 (Source: https://community.secondlife.com/knowledgebase/english/accessing-adult-land-and-content-r236/). Ofcom's draft guidance treats debit cards and self-declaration as weak, so this would not satisfy the OSA (Source: https://community.secondlife.com/forums/topic/518772-new-uk-censorship-requirements/).
- **Separate adult settlement**: no virtual world precedent found; the closest analogues are adult-specialist high-risk acquirers (Segpay, CCBill, Epoch) that operate under MCC 5967 at Tier-1 fees. No cost-in-users data exists for a split-rail design because no UGC world has tried it.

## Design implications for nolife

1. **Treat adult/romantic roleplay as a first-class purpose, and measure it.** SL never published an adult share, which left it unable to defend or price that economy. nolife should instrument maturity-tagged spend, dwell time and cash-out from day one.
2. **Keep the licensed rail at one remove from content.** SL survived 2025 because its processor sells "virtual currency", not adult goods. nolife's cash-out rail should settle a platform currency under KYC, with no adult MCC on the consumer-facing purchase; otherwise Stripe is unusable and Adyen only with approval.
3. **Build Tier-1-grade controls anyway, pre-emptively**: uploader verification before Adult-tagged goods go live, consent records for likeness-based content, pre-publication review for Adult listings, and transaction monitoring. These are what AN 5196 and VIRP require and are the price of not being the next itch.io.
4. **Age-verified sub-wallet is the cleanest architecture.** A verified-adult status (Persona-style or estimation-based, free in the UK) that unlocks both Adult regions and the ability to buy or sell Adult-tagged goods lets nolife satisfy the OSA without gating the whole world, and lets adult goods be excluded from the *general* cash-out pool if a processor ever demands it.
5. **Do not plan on excluding adult goods from cash-out as a default.** The Tumblr precedent (~30% traffic loss, distress sale) shows the downside of a blanket exit; the OnlyFans reversal shows the schemes will accept adult UGC once controls exist. Hold exclusion as a contingency tied to a specific processor demand, with a parallel adult-specialist acquirer pre-qualified.
6. **Deindexing is a survivable middle path.** itch.io's model (hide Adult from discovery, keep direct links and sales) preserved creators' income while satisfying processors; nolife's Marketplace should be able to flip Adult listings to opt-in-only discovery in hours.
