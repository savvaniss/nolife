# Design panel: six independent proposals

Before the unified design was written, six independent proposals for nolife were drafted from deliberately different angles. Each one read the same failure taxonomy (`docs/research/failure-taxonomy.md`) and research briefs, answered the same fourteen numbered sections, and ended with a coverage table for requirements R1-R30.

| Proposal | Angle |
|---|---|
| `proposal-community-sociologist.md` | Purpose first: roles, institutions, scarcity, rituals and self-governance. |
| `proposal-platform-economist.md` | Creator economy first: money flows, take rates, cash-out and portability. |
| `proposal-engine-architect.md` | Fidelity first: engine, graphics tiers, asset pipeline and performance budgets. |
| `proposal-ai-world-designer.md` | Living world first: disclosed AI inhabitants, ecology and emergent story under strict guardrails. |
| `proposal-open-web-architect.md` | Open protocol first: federation, portable identity and legacy import. |
| `proposal-studio-exec.md` | Survival first: wedge communities, sequencing, funding and what to cut. |

Three judges scored every proposal on seven criteria through different lenses (root causes, feasibility, desirability). Their reports are in `judges/`. The unified design in `docs/design/` takes one proposal as its base and grafts the strongest ideas from the others; its decision log records what was adopted, rejected and why.

These proposals are inputs, not the design. Where a proposal disagrees with `docs/design/`, the design documents win.
