# What the winners did right

**Summary.** Nine platforms did what Second Life could not: they grew past a few hundred thousand people or held a community for two decades. None of them is a Second Life. Roblox and Fortnite supply purpose through games and pay creators from engagement pools worth, respectively, more than $1.5 billion in 2025 and $1 billion cumulative by 2026. GTA RP sells scarce seats on curated servers; FFXIV makes a house something you can lose; Habbo never re-issues a rare; VRChat lets an off-platform avatar economy in Japan carry its growth; Zepeto proved avatar-first mobile reach and then showed how fast a teen cohort evaporates; Discord became the social graph every world rents. Each solves onboarding by being free, instant and reachable on a phone, and each has retreated from pure engagement pay toward direct sales with a published split. What none offers is one persistent self with owned land, in-world creation, adult cash-out and 18+ space designed in rather than retrofitted. That gap, not their scale, is where nolife lives.

## How to read this document

This is the reader-facing version of section 4 of the project's failure taxonomy, written from the project's research briefs (winners-lessons, community-purpose, business-models, plus the safety, networking and regional-law briefs), their fact-check verdicts and the three critiques; no new web research was done. Every number carries an inline citation to the page the project's search extracts surfaced. Page fetches were blocked throughout, so "verified" means a search extract matched the claim, not that the page was read; where the search-backed checkers corrected a brief, the corrected value is used, and figures resting on prior knowledge or a single secondary site are labelled. Requirement IDs (R6, R8 and so on) refer to section 6 of the taxonomy.

The product under design is nolife. Its predecessor is Second Life, operated by Linden Lab.

One caveat governs everything below: these nine were selected on outcome. Rec Room met every criterion here in 2021 (150 million lifetime players, UGC everywhere, a working payout) and announced its shutdown for 1 June 2026 after keeping about 30 cents of each UGC dollar ([Rec Room](https://blog.recroom.com/posts/2025/8/28/rec-room-finances-and-ugc-revenue)). Every principle below is at most necessary, never sufficient.

## 2. Roblox: the scale benchmark, and what the benchmark costs

### 2.1 The numbers

In the quarter ended 30 June 2026 Roblox reported 123 million daily active users (+10% year on year), 29 billion hours engaged, revenue of about $1.5 billion (+36%), bookings of $1.6 billion (+8%), a net loss of $185 million, average bookings per DAU of $12.66 (-2%) and 27 million monthly unique payers ([SEC 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001315098/000162828026051059/ex991-robloxq22026earnin.htm)). That DAU figure is the third sequential decline from the Q3 2025 peak: 152 million, 144 million, 132 million, 123 million ([Shacknews](https://www.shacknews.com/article/150208/roblox-daus-grew-10-percent-yoy)). Management guided Q3 2026 bookings to $1.58-1.65 billion, a 14-18% year-on-year decline, blaming a shift from the viral titles of 2025 to lower-monetising evergreen games and a recommendation change that favours retention ([Quartr](https://quartr.com/events/roblox-rblx-q4-2025_ozGB34Pf); [Shacknews](https://www.shacknews.com/article/150208/roblox-daus-grew-10-percent-yoy)).

The growth that remains is adult and non-American: DAU and hours among users 18 and over grew 32% and 27% in Q2 2026, with Japan (+67%) and India (+64%) leading, per secondary coverage rather than the SEC exhibit ([Outlook India](https://respawn.outlookindia.com/gaming/gaming-news/roblox-q2-2026-revenue-soars-36-to-15b-despite-bookings-miss)); a year earlier regional pricing had lifted paying users 26% in Brazil and 52% in the Philippines ([Roblox](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2025/04/roblox-launches-regional-pricing-for-in-experience-items)).

FY2024 closed at revenue of $3,602 million, bookings of $4,369 million, a net loss of $935 million and average DAU of 82.9 million ([BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250206476916/en)). Concurrency records belong to 2025: a two-game crossover on 23 August 2025 pushed platform-wide concurrency to about 47.3 million, above Steam's record, and Steal a Brainrot alone reached 25.4 million on 7 October 2025 ([Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/?p=188004); [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/robloxs-steal-a-brainrot-becomes-first-game-to-surpass-25m-concurrent-players)), all in servers of at most 200 public players; a 700-player tier can no longer be joined publicly ([Roblox DevForum](https://devforum.roblox.com/t/is-it-still-possible-to-opt-into-700-player-servers/4210626)).

### 2.2 How it pays creators

Developer Exchange payouts were $741 million in 2023, $922.8 million in 2024 and more than $1.5 billion in 2025; Q4 2025 alone was $477 million, 33.7% of revenue, and the top 1,000 creators averaged about $1.3 million, a call figure not found in a search extract ([Quartr](https://quartr.com/events/roblox-rblx-q4-2025_ozGB34Pf); [SEC 8-K Q4 2025](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1315098/000131509826000009/ex991-q42025shareholder.htm); [BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250206476916/en)). The cash-out rate rose 8.5% from $0.0035 to $0.0038 per Robux on 5 September 2025, with a 30,000-Robux minimum ([Fintool](https://fintool.com/app/research/companies/RBLX/earnings/Q4%202025)). Sources disagree on that date: two no-search checkers recalled September 2023, but earnings commentary attributes Q4 2025 DevEx growth to the 8.5% rise, so 2025 is used here. On 8 June 2026 a 42% uplift took effect for eligible spend (game passes, subscriptions, private servers) by age-checked US players 18 and over, raising the effective creator share from 26.6% to 37.8% in games on the R15 avatar framework ([Roblox](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/04/roblox-fuels-high-fidelity-games-over-18-players-increases-qualifying-devex-rate-42)). Outside that uplift, Epic's own comparison puts the Roblox creator take at about 25% of in-experience spend, because app-store fees leave before anyone is paid ([GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/fortnite/)).

### 2.3 How it pays for safety

Roblox's infrastructure plus trust-and-safety line was about $915 million in 2024 and $1,153.5 million in 2025, roughly $0.92 per DAU per month in 2024, about as much as it pays creators ([EDGAR tools](https://app.edgar.tools/companies/RBLX); [Roblox 10-K FY2025](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1315098/000131509826000024/rblx-20251231.htm)). Age checks for chat went global in January 2026, sorting users into six bands with adjacent-band chat by default; 45% of DAUs had completed the check by 31 January 2026 ([Roblox](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/01/roblox-age-checks-required-to-chat); [Quartr](https://quartr.com/events/roblox-rblx-q4-2025_ozGB34Pf)). The checks followed state lawsuits, and settlements followed the checks: $35.8 million with three states in April 2026 ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-04-21/roblox-settles-with-states-for-35-8-million-over-child-safety)).

The lesson for nolife is in the Q2 2026 miss: when Roblox tuned its recommender toward retention, per-hour monetisation fell, the trade-off R8 makes explicit by weighting the creator pool on returning purchasing users rather than raw hours.

## 3. Fortnite and UEFN: the engagement pool, now hybridised with direct sales

Creator Economy 2.0 launched on 22 March 2023 and puts 40% of Fortnite's net revenue from the Item Shop and most real-money purchases into a monthly engagement pool shared across all islands, Epic's own included ([Fortnite](https://www.fortnite.com/news/introducing-the-creator-economy-2-0)). Annual payouts for 2024 are reported as both $352 million and $320 million; the sources disagree and Epic has published only a cumulative figure ([Everybody](https://everybody.beehiiv.com/p/the-business-of-fortnite); [esports.gg](https://esports.gg/guides/fortnite/how-do-fortnite-creators-make-money/)). That cumulative figure, $1 billion paid to UEFN creators, was announced at Unreal Fest Chicago on 17 June 2026; one secondary site dates the crossing to January 2026 ([GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/epic-games-has-paid-out-1-billion-to-creators-since-launching-unreal-editor-for-fortnite-in-2023/)). Creator islands held 47% of player hours in May 2026, up from 38% a year earlier (the winners brief's "35%" was corrected by the search-backed checker), and more than 75% of in-game transactions now happen outside Battle Royale (same source).

The pool has been rewritten twice in a year. From 1 November 2025 only players who have made at least one purchase count toward engagement, retention is measured per island, and a creator who brings a new or six-month-lapsed player receives 75% of that player's pool contribution for six months ([Fortnite](https://www.fortnite.com/news/fortnite-developers-will-soon-be-able-to-sell-in-game-items)). A paid "Sponsored Row" launched on 24 November 2025 with 50% of its revenue returned to the pool ([VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/fortnite-will-soon-let-island-creators-sell-in-game-items-and-pay-to-appear-in-a-new-sponsored-row)). Then direct sales arrived: publishing of in-island items opened on 9 January 2026, with creators keeping 100% of V-Bucks value (about 74% of the cash a player spent, after 12-30% store fees) through 31 January 2027 and 50% thereafter ([Fortnite](https://www.fortnite.com/news/fortnite-developers-will-soon-be-able-to-sell-in-game-items)). By June 2026 more than 3,000 islands earned more from in-island transactions than from the pool ([GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/epic-games-has-paid-out-1-billion-to-creators-since-launching-unreal-editor-for-fortnite-in-2023/)).

Fortnite also owns the shared "when". Marshmello drew 10.7 million concurrent in February 2019 (a prior-knowledge figure not re-found by search) ([PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/fortnites-dj-marshmello-concert-surpasses-games-concurrent-record-with-107-players)); Travis Scott's "Astronomical" reached 12.3 million on 23 April 2020 ([Tubefilter](https://tubefilter.com/2020/04/24/travis-scott-fortnite-concert-all-time-record/)); the Galactus event of 1 December 2020 reached 15.3 million, still the largest in-game event, though some sources misdate it to 2021 ([Music Business Worldwide](https://musicbusinessworldwide.com/fortnites-remix-the-finale-concert-draws-over-14m-players-breaking-records)); and "Remix: The Finale" on 30 November 2024 drew more than 14 million concurrent plus about 3 million stream viewers ([Music Business Worldwide](https://musicbusinessworldwide.com/fortnites-remix-the-finale-concert-draws-over-14m-players-breaking-records)). Every one of those millions stood in a lobby of at most 100, per Epic's own replication documentation ([Epic](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/iris-replication-system-in-unreal-engine)). Second Life's 100-avatar region cap ([Second Life wiki](https://wiki.secondlife.com/wiki/Land)) is not what stops it holding events; the absence of a calendar is.

The lesson is convergence: Epic began with a pure engagement pool and added direct sales; Roblox began with direct sales and added an engagement-weighted bonus; each formula changed when the old one was farmed. R8 copies the purchaser-only weighting, acquisition bounty and published formula, but sizes the pool as a share of platform net revenue, because the taxonomy's section 5 arithmetic shows a Linden-scale world cannot fund 40% of gross spend.

## 4. Minecraft: ownership and a self-hosted server economy (weakly verified)

Minecraft is the only platform here that sells a copy and then lets owners host the world themselves. The last official sales figure is 300 million copies, announced at Minecraft Live in October 2023; the 325-400 million copies and 212-222 million MAU circulating for 2025-26 are aggregator estimates, not Microsoft's ([SQ Magazine](https://sqmagazine.co.uk/minecraft-statistics/)). The curated Bedrock Marketplace launched in June 2017; its twelve launch partners earned $1 million in the first three months, and Mojang described the creator share as a "majority" of what remains after the 30% app-store cut ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2017/09/05/minecraft-marketplace-microsoft-million)). The widely repeated "$500 million cumulative" and "about 70% creator share" come from secondary analyses with no Microsoft statement found ([Corq](https://corq.studio/insights/minecraft-marketplace-inside-the-platforms-500-million-creator-economy/)). The Java server economy (a 2014 EULA permitting cosmetics and ranks but not pay-to-win) rests on prior knowledge and was not searched.

The lesson is structural: self-hosting means the operator cannot kill a community by turning off a server, the leading cause of death in the project's successor table. R20 (self-hostable cells, escrowed images, export) descends from this.

## 5. VRChat: avatar identity, earned trust and an economy it does not own

VRChat publishes no regular metrics, so its numbers are staff posts and third-party trackers. On those, it is the only social VR world that scaled: 136,567 concurrent on 1 January 2025, 148,886 on 1 January 2026, normal weekend peaks of 120,000-125,000, and a record of 158,192 at a Japanese-language virtual concert in the first quarter of 2026 ([Road to VR](https://www.roadtovr.com/vrchat-new-record-new-years-eve-2025-2026/); [Road to VR](https://roadtovr.com/vrchat-key-stats-japan-growth-concurrents/); [Mogura VR](https://www.moguravr.com/vrchat-concurrent-users-record-2026/)). Sources disagree on the record's date: Road to VR ties it to the Kaguya concert of 15 February 2026 (corrected from 156,716), while a VRChat wiki chronology puts it on 8 March 2026 at Sanrio Virtual Festival. The Steam-only peak was 79,593 on 15 February 2026 ([SteamDB](https://steamcommunity.com/groups/SteamDB/announcements/detail/7285229827084255392)), so roughly half the audience arrives through other launchers. All of it is reached through instances of a few dozen; the "40 default, 80 cap" figure is community lore, unverified.

About 30% of staff were laid off in June 2024, citing pandemic-era over-hiring ([Road to VR](https://roadtovr.com/vrchat-lays-off-30-of-company/)). Its own creator economy, opened to avatars in May 2025, splits spend roughly 50% to the creator, 30% to the store platform and 20% to VRChat and its payout processor, with credits paying out at about $0.005 each, a 30,000-credit minimum and a 1.5% fee ([VRChat](https://creators.vrchat.com/economy/welcome-to-the-ce); [VRChat](https://creators.vrchat.com/economy/payout)). The larger economy is one it never built: Japan is VRChat's largest web market, at 37% of official-site visits against 27% for the US in Sensor Tower's 2025 data ([Sensor Tower](https://sensortower.com/ja/blog/vrchat-high-popularity-in-japan-JP)); pixiv's BOOTH 3D-model category passed 10 billion yen in annual transactions in 2025 ([Real Sound](https://realsound.jp/tech/2026/02/post-2303955.html)); and in September 2026 pixiv took an equity stake in VRChat Inc. ([GameBusiness.jp](https://www.gamebusiness.jp/article/2026/09/09/27973.html)). Full iOS and Android apps shipped in October 2025, with Japan taking 25-26% of early downloads ([Mogura VR](https://www.moguravr.com/?p=312262)).

Two mechanisms transfer. Trust is earned by behaviour: a Trust Rank ladder (Visitor, New User, User, Known, Trusted, plus a visible Nuisance flag) gates upload and reporting rights and is explicitly not raised by idling, low-effort uploads or mass-friending ([VRChat docs](https://docs.vrchat.com/docs/vrchat-safety-and-trust-system)). And VRChat verifies adults without serving adult content: optional 18+ verification through Persona reached all VRChat Plus subscribers on 28 January 2025, unlocks 18+ instances and, per the VRChat wiki only, gates suggestive content for unverified UK users, while pornography stays banned platform-wide ([VRChat wiki](https://wiki.vrchat.com/wiki/Age_Assurance)). R23 adopts the first; R21 and R22 build the gated adult district VRChat chose not to. Its weakness is instructive too: the Easy Anti-Cheat update drew 22,000 protest votes in 24 hours, a trust shock a world with no persistence and no land could absorb and a world with both could not ([Dazed](https://www.dazeddigital.com/science-tech/article/56662/1/why-thousands-of-users-are-abandoning-the-worlds-most-popular-vr-game)).

## 6. GTA RP, FiveM and NoPixel: roleplay as the purpose engine, now first-party

FiveM is a third-party client for GTA V that Rockstar banned in 2015 and hired in 2023: on 11 August 2023 Rockstar announced that Cfx.re, the FiveM and RedM team, had joined the company, terms undisclosed ([Game World Observer](https://gameworldobserver.com/2023/08/11/cfx-re-joins-rockstar-deal-fivem-redm-roleplay)). The monetisation rules came separately (a roleplay-server policy last updated 7 January 2025 and a Creator Platform License Agreement rewritten on 12 January 2026, the day an official Cfx Marketplace opened): servers may sell cosmetics and queue priority but not in-game currency or gameplay advantage, real-world brands need licences, and payments run through Tebex, which one vendor calls the "exclusive" partner and another merely the default at a 15% fee ([GameServerKings](https://www.gameserverkings.com/knowledge-base/fivem/server-monetisation/)).

Scale is measurable only on Steam: an all-time peak of 221,062 in May 2026, after 202,756 on 15 March 2026, at which point FiveM out-peaked GTA V itself ([Steam Charts](https://steamcharts.com/app/2676230); [players.com.ua](https://players.com.ua/en/news/fivem-surpasses-gta-5-in-player-count-over-the-past-week/)). The purpose engine is the whitelisted server. NoPixel admits players by written application ("what is your definition of roleplay?" plus in-character scenarios), held 32 players per instance in 2021 guides, and opens a paid donator whitelist when applications close ([Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/gta/how-join-nopixel-gta-5-rp-server-a-step-by-step-guide)). GTA V was Twitch's most-watched category in 2021 at 2.1 billion hours, with xQc routinely drawing 150,000-plus viewers from inside NoPixel ([Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/gta/gta-5-ends-2021-watched-game-twitch); [Business Insider](https://businessinsider.nl/a-grand-theft-auto-v-roleplay-server-called-nopixel-continues-to-dominate-twitch-with-its-immersive-ecosystem/)). NoPixel 4.0 launched on 15 December 2023 ([RockstarIntel](https://rockstarintel.com/gta-rp-server-nopixel-4-update-release-date/)); its own price list did not surface, but comparable whitelisted servers charge $25, $50, $100 and $200 a month for priority tiers, or $8.99-14.99 at the low end, all "subject to server capacity" ([Tebex](https://ascenddev.tebex.io/category/priority-roles); [Tebex](https://enigmashop.tebex.io/category/enigma-queue-priorit)).

Scarcity of seats plus curation is the product: people pay $200 a month for the right to queue for a job, a faction and a story told to a stream audience. Two cautions: the loop rides on GTA V's content, and GTA VI ships on 19 November 2026 with no public word on roleplay tooling ([Guru3D](https://www.guru3d.com/story/rockstar-delays-gta-vi-again-new-release-date-set-for-november-2026/)); and the steelman critique counts thousands of empty FiveM servers for every NoPixel, so the seat cap is a feature of a few curated estates, not a scaling model. R17 copies the mechanism for the roleplay estates Second Life already runs on access lists and HUD meters.

## 7. FFXIV: engineered scarcity and the venue nobody designed

Final Fantasy XIV passed 30 million registered players, a count that includes free-trial accounts, as announced at Fan Festival Tokyo in January 2024 ([GamesMEA](https://gamesmea.com/final-fantasy-xiv-online-now-has-over-30-million-players)), and Dawntrail in July 2024 produced the highest concurrency since A Realm Reborn ([Game World Observer](https://gameworldobserver.com/2024/07/05/final-fantasy-14-record-concurrent-players-dawntrail)). It charges from $12.99 a month ([Subger](https://subger.com/en/service/ffxiv-sub)), which places it among the paywalled survivors that refute "paywall at entry is fatal".

Its contribution is housing. Since patch 6.1 (first lottery window 12 April 2022, a prior-knowledge date) every plot goes by lottery with the full plot price as the ticket, one entry per period and four days to confirm; one wiki says a 50% deposit, but full price is the documented rule ([Inverse](https://www.inverse.com/gaming/final-fantasy-14-housing-updates-lottery-empyreum-patch-6-1)). A house not entered for 45 days is auto-demolished with warnings from day 30 and an 80% gil refund; one guide says 30 days, the patch-note-based sources say 45 ([TechRaptor](https://techraptor.net/gaming/news/ffxiv-house-demolition-suspension-reinstated-temporarily); [ExpertBeacon](https://expertbeacon.com/how-long-does-it-take-to-lose-your-house-in-ff14/)). The timer has been suspended repeatedly (the pandemic, the war in Ukraine, a North American pause in early 2025) and resumed on NA data centres on 6 June 2025, with relinquished plots purchasable from 22 July ([TechRaptor](https://techraptor.net/gaming/news/ffxiv-house-demolition-suspension-reinstated-temporarily)). Forum anecdotes report 75 consecutive losses and roughly 3% odds, with about 3,500 bids on a batch of plots ([Square Enix forum](https://forum.square-enix.com/ffxiv/threads/523854)).

Scarcity of place is extreme, and the scene exists anyway: players turned housing into nightclubs and cafés the designers never intended, builders charge up to about 50 million gil per venue, and some build in out-of-bounds void space ([Medium](https://medium.com/@deirdre.darkk/beginners-guide-to-the-ffxiv-nightclub-scene-9efa7d823cfd)). The venues are promoted on Discord and depend on grey-zone client mods (prior knowledge, unsearched). R16 takes the mechanism and adds a published pause policy, because FFXIV's repeated suspensions show the rule costs goodwill every time the world outside intrudes; what Second Life lacked, as the causal critique notes, was legible scarcity, not finite land.

## 8. Habbo: 25 years of pixel persistence

Habbo's loop is the rare: limited-time furniture that is never re-released. Its official marketplace update logged 4,373,036 trades worth 110,147,058 credits ([Habbo](https://www.habbo.com/community/article/22292/update-state-habbo-economy)), and community lore records what happens when scarcity breaks: the 2004 "Palsternakka" incident, when rares were accidentally re-sold on a foreign catalogue page, crashed values and drove players out ([Habbox forum](https://habboxforum.com/showthread.php?t=701469)). In 2012 the company cut 25% of staff and closed all eleven country offices in February, four months before Channel 4's exposé on adult predators led to "The Great Mute" across every hotel ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2012/06/12/trouble-at-habbo-hotel-balderton-dumps-13-stake-in-owner-sulake-over-dodgy-child-content/); [Gurl World](https://gurlworld.com/what-happened-to-habbo-habbo-hotel-turns-25/)). The search-backed checker corrected the reading that the scandal gutted the company: the contraction came first, and the safer lesson is that a safety scandal is most lethal to a company already shrinking.

Azerion took a controlling stake in 2018 and fully acquired Sulake in January 2021, when Habbo had 800,000-plus monthly actives across 115 countries ([PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/azerion-fully-acquires-habbo-hotel-dev-sulake)); nothing later is broken out of Azerion's EUR 540.6 million 2025 group revenue ([GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/04/17/3276396/0/en/azerion-group-publishes-its-2025-annual-report.html)). The move that matters most to nolife came on 18 June 2024: Habbo Hotel: Origins, a six-month restoration of the 2005 client from "an old decrepit server with some long-lost files", launched as an 18+ hotel with no casinos or gambling and an in-world voting forum ([80.lv](https://80.lv/articles/the-original-2005-habbo-hotel-opens-its-doors-later-today); [GGRecon](https://ggrecon.com/articles/habbo-hotel-fans-have-a-major-issue-with-the-games-return)). Origins inherited its community by preserving what the community remembered, where Linden Lab's Sansar inherited nothing by building something new. R18 (charter import, OAR/IAR import, a chat bridge with Second Life) is written to that contrast.

## 9. Zepeto: avatar-first, mobile-first, and gone quiet

Naver Z's press release of 4 March 2022 is the last reliable snapshot: more than 300 million registered users, 20 million monthly actives, about 95% of them outside Korea, 2 million creators on ZEPETO Studio, 68 million creator items sold and 2.3 billion items traded ([KED Global](https://m.kedglobal.com/newsView/ked202203040009)). By August 2022 Naver still cited 20 million MAU, with more than 5 billion content creations and 3 billion item sales ([TYN Magazine](https://tynmagazine.com/asias-largest-metaverse-platform-zepeto-ramps-up-global-expansion/)). The "340 million registered", "2.83 million creators" and "400 million users" figures that circulate were not found in any search extract and are treated here as unverified.

The fragility showed as soon as the pandemic ended. Korean weekly users fell to their lowest point since mid-2021 in May 2022 as teenagers went back to school; the reported figure of 133,708 comes from a single news report and was not re-found by the checker ([Asia News Network](https://asianews.network/number-of-metaverse-users-hits-lowest-point-after-social-distancing-rules-lifted)). A Preqin profile shows revenue of about $46.3 million against EBITDA of about -$42.5 million for an unstated year, and Naver deconsolidated Naver Z in 2024 ([Preqin](https://www.preqin.com/data/profile/asset/zepeto/394765); [Quartr](https://quartr.com/events/naver-corporation-035420-q1-2025_FLDNba4j)). No 2024-26 MAU, revenue or restructuring disclosure surfaced in English-language search.

Zepeto proves an avatar-first, creator-dense world can reach 20 million monthly actives on phones with no land and no scripting, and that a cohort whose purpose was "somewhere to be during lockdown" leaves when the lockdown does. R14 takes the mobile reach; R11 and R15 supply the schedule and roles Zepeto never did.

## 10. Discord: the social layer every world rents

Discord has more than 200 million monthly users, a figure repeated in a December 2025 statement ([Fintool](https://fintool.com/news/discord-ipo-confidential-filing)); third parties estimate about $725 million of 2024 revenue, mostly Nitro, against a $15 billion private valuation in 2021 and secondary pricing near $8.5 billion in June 2026 ([TechStackIPO](https://techstackipo.com/ipo/discord)). It filed confidentially for a US IPO on 7 January 2026 with Goldman Sachs and JPMorgan; no public S-1 has followed ([Fintool](https://fintool.com/news/discord-ipo-confidential-filing)). Its developer surface is now a platform: the Social SDK for in-game voice, chat and friend graphs went into closed beta on 17 March 2025 and opened to all developers on 13 August 2025 ([Discord](https://discord.com/press-releases/social-sdk-ingame-communications); [GameFromScratch](https://gamefromscratch.com/discord-launch-game-development-sdk/)).

Every winner here depends on it. NoPixel's whitelist, FFXIV's venue listings, VRChat's event calendars and Roblox communities all schedule, moderate and recruit on Discord rather than in-world. Its churn is known only through vendor claims (most servers lose more than half of new joiners within seven days; "83% of new servers never pass 100 members"), which the community-purpose checker flags as unverified marketing ([YellowWorm](https://yellowworm.io/blog/discord-member-retention)).

The dependency now carries policy risk. A third-party support vendor was compromised for 58 hours on 22-24 September 2025, exposing about 70,000 government-ID images uploaded for age appeals; attackers claimed 2.1 million IDs, which Discord disputes ([CyberInsider](https://cyberinsider.com/discord-confirms-70000-government-ids-exposed-in-third-party-breach/)). A "teen-by-default" global age-assurance model was announced in February 2026, delayed from March after privacy backlash, and reportedly began rolling out on 22 September 2026, with more than 90% of users bucketed automatically by behavioural signals and on-device facial estimation for the rest ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/02/09/discord-to-roll-out-age-verification-next-month-for-full-access-to-its-platform/); [TNW](https://thenextweb.com/news/discord-global-age-checks-teen-default)). Discord tells developers no SDK changes are needed, but Social SDK studios must still build their own safeguards for age-restricted users ([Discord](https://support-dev.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/38338398970263-Upcoming-Discord-Age-Assurance-Changes-What-You-Need-To-Know)). Australia's under-16 ban exempts Discord and Roblox only because their "primary purpose" is messaging or gaming, and eSafety says the list is not static ([eSafety](https://www.esafety.gov.au/about-us/industry-regulation/social-media-age-restrictions/faqs)).

## 11. The cross-cut: purpose, onboarding, creator pay

### 11.1 How each supplies purpose

Roblox and Fortnite sell a goal and a shared "when"; Minecraft, ownership of the world itself; VRChat, being someone, somewhere, with others; GTA RP, a role people are waiting for you to play; FFXIV and Habbo, status through scarce place and scarce objects; Zepeto, self-presentation; Discord, the group's memory and schedule. The community-purpose brief's conclusion holds across all nine: purpose is supplied by other people with a reason to meet at a time and place, and the platform's job is to supply the roles, the schedule and the scarcity that make the meeting worth keeping. Second Life supplied places and tools; its staffed venues worked and its unstaffed corporate builds, "a shop in the High Street filled with brochures but never having any staff", did not ([TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/news/internet/whatever-happened-to-second-life-1030314/2)). None of the nine leaves purpose to the user. The taxonomy restates this as a hypothesis to measure (R11), because the causal effect of group membership on retention is held by Bungie, Square Enix and Linden Lab and published by none.

### 11.2 How each solves onboarding

Every platform that scaled is free to start and reachable from a phone or a browser. Roblox and Fortnite land a new account in a game within a minute of install; VRChat and Zepeto are app-store downloads; Habbo Origins and Discord open in a browser; FiveM and Minecraft ask for a purchase and then put the player on a server where other people already are. Second Life reports that about 7% of arrivals are retained and that its mobile client is still in beta ([Modemworld](https://modemworld.me/tag/project-zero/)); the taxonomy notes 7-10% at 90 days is the industry base rate, so the funnel is not what stalled Second Life. What the winners add is density on arrival, and GTA RP makes onboarding a filter: the application essay is the first act of roleplay. R11 (first hour = first group), R12 (density SLO) and R26 (a furnished home and dressed avatar before any scripting panel) are the transfers.

### 11.3 How each pays creators

| World | Mechanism | Creator share of consumer spend | Minimum / rail |
|---|---|---|---|
| Roblox | DevEx at $0.0038 per Robux; 42% uplift on verified-adult US spend | ~25% typical; 26.6% to 37.8% on uplifted spend ([Roblox](https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/04/roblox-fuels-high-fidelity-games-over-18-players-increases-qualifying-devex-rate-42); [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/fortnite/)) | 30,000 Robux; 13+, ID-verified |
| Fortnite | 40% engagement pool (purchasers only, acquisition bounty) plus in-island sales | Pool shared with Epic's own modes; ~74% of cash on direct sales to Jan 2027, ~37% after ([Fortnite](https://www.fortnite.com/news/fortnite-developers-will-soon-be-able-to-sell-in-game-items)) | Epic payout profile |
| Minecraft | Curated Marketplace | "Majority" after 30% store cut ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2017/09/05/minecraft-marketplace-microsoft-million)) | Partner programme |
| VRChat | Credits, Avatar Marketplace | ~50% ([VRChat](https://creators.vrchat.com/economy/welcome-to-the-ce)) | 30,000 credits, 1.5% fee |
| FiveM | Server-side sales via Tebex | Server keeps net of Tebex fee; no platform cash-out ([GameServerKings](https://www.gameserverkings.com/knowledge-base/fivem/server-monetisation/)) | Tebex |
| Second Life | L$ sales, LindeX cash-out | Users estimate the all-in take at 20-31% against "90/10" marketing; ~$78M paid in 2023 ([Modemworld](https://modemworld.me/2024/12/20/)) | Tilia/Thunes, 1099-K threshold |

Three regularities. The 30% store tax decides creator share: Rec Room kept 30 cents of a UGC dollar and shut down, and Roblox's 25% exists because 20%-plus of bookings leave as store fees first ([Rec Room](https://blog.recroom.com/posts/2025/8/28/rec-room-finances-and-ugc-revenue)). Pools are power-law and get farmed: Roblox and Epic both re-engineered formulas in 2025-26 to count only purchasers and verified adults. And a pool alone is not enough: Meta's $50 million Horizon Creator Fund paid fee-free bonuses on time spent, retention and purchases, and Horizon never passed 200,000 monthly users ([Meta](https://developers.meta.com/horizon/blog/gdc-2025-horizon-worlds-create-earn-bonuses-desktop-editor-tools); [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/177471/meta-falls-short-of-user-goal-for-horizon-worlds-wsj)). R7 (own billing, a published fee stack with the platform's margin beside it) and R8 (a pool of 20-30% of platform net revenue, 10-20% reserved for venues) follow from the first two; the third is why R8 is paired with R15's roles and calendar.

## 12. What each still lacks, and what a Second-Life-like world can offer

| World | What it does not offer | The gap nolife can fill |
|---|---|---|
| Roblox | Owned land; adult content; cash-out only for 13+ ID-verified creators; hit-dependent DAU | A persistent home and an adult economy for the 18+ cohort Roblox is only now pricing for |
| Fortnite | Persistence; identity beyond cosmetics; any event larger than a 100-player lobby | Continuity between events: a place that is still there on Tuesday |
| Minecraft | A shared identity or economy across servers; adults as a designed audience | One self across many self-hosted cells, with cash-out |
| VRChat | Persistent land; published metrics; an on-platform economy; adult content beyond verification | Owned space, a measured world, and the gated adult district VRChat declined to build |
| GTA RP | A world of its own (it rides GTA V); cash-out; seats beyond a few curated servers | The seat-capped estate as a platform feature, with a real economy under it |
| FFXIV | UGC; cash-out; a sanctioned venue scene (it runs on grey-zone mods) | Scarce public land plus unlimited private creation, with venues as first-class objects |
| Habbo | An economy that pays out; 3D; any disclosed scale since 2021 | Scarcity mechanics on a world people can build in |
| Zepeto | Retention past a demand shock; purpose beyond appearance; disclosed numbers since 2022 | Schedule and roles under an avatar-first mobile funnel |
| Discord | A world at all; identity it will not re-gate; freedom from IPO-era policy drift | A native social layer the world owns, bridged to the graph people already have |

The taxonomy puts the common gap in one sentence: none offers one persistent self with portable inventory, owned land, in-world creation, adult cash-out and 18+ spaces designed in rather than retrofitted; the only worlds covering all four pillars today are Resonite, at about 170-200 Steam concurrent ([Steambase](https://steambase.io/games/resonite)), and the OpenSim hypergrid, at about 45,000-48,000 monthly actives ([Hypergrid Business](https://www.hypergridbusiness.com/2026/05/opensim-users-land-area-both-up-this-month/)). The steelman critique's reply travels with this: the market for that bundle may be 0.5-1 million people, most of them already in Second Life. The winners' adult turn is the strongest evidence against that reading: Roblox's 18+ users are its fastest-growing cohort with a 42% payout uplift, Habbo relaunched as 18+, VRChat verifies adults, Discord has moved to age bands, and each approaches from below the audience Second Life has held for twenty years with none of its economy, land or creation tools.

## 13. Where the sources disagree

- Roblox's DevEx rate rise to $0.0038 is dated 5 September 2025 by earnings commentary and September 2023 by two no-search checkers' recollection; 2025, the search-verified date, is used.
- Fortnite's 2024 creator payout is reported as $352 million and $320 million by different secondary sites; Epic has published only the $1 billion cumulative figure.
- The Galactus event is dated December 2020 by most sources and 2021 by some; 1 December 2020 is correct.
- VRChat's 158,192 record is placed on 15 February 2026 (Kaguya concert) by Road to VR and on 8 March 2026 (Sanrio Virtual Festival) by a wiki chronology; VRChat publishes no official figure.
- FFXIV's demolition timer is 45 days in patch-note-based sources and 30 in one guide; the lottery ticket is full plot price in most sources and a 50% deposit in one wiki.
- Tebex is FiveM's "exclusive" payment partner per one vendor and the "default" at a 15% fee per a competitor; the licence text could not be read.
- Discord's "200M+ MAU" is sometimes dated April 2025, but the statement search found is from December 2025, which is the date used ([Fintool](https://fintool.com/news/discord-ipo-confidential-filing)).
- Zepeto's 340 million registered users and 2.83 million creators appear only in aggregators; Naver's own March 2022 figures (300 million, 2 million) are used.

## 14. What this means for nolife

1. **Roles and loops in the first hour, not an orientation island (R11, R15).** Every winner supplies a goal, a seat or a schedule before the user must invent one; test it with a randomised D30 holdout, since no platform has published the causal effect.
2. **Lead with the adult market the winners are discovering late (R21, R22).** Roblox prices verified-adult spend 42% higher, Habbo Origins is 18+, VRChat verifies and then bans; nolife alone is designing the gated adult district with an economy under it, on the licensed rail Second Life has run for years.
3. **Hybrid creator economics with a published split (R7, R8).** Copy Epic's purchaser-only pool and 75% acquisition bounty, pay a stated share on direct sales, own billing on web and PC so the 30% store tax does not set the number, and state the platform's required margin, because Rec Room died with a creator-friendly split.
4. **Scarce shared land, unlimited private space, a pause policy (R16).** FFXIV's lottery and 45-day rule make place a status object; its suspensions show the rule needs a published exception path.
5. **Seat-capped, whitelisted roleplay estates with paid priority and no pay-to-win (R17).** Adopt Rockstar's rules wholesale and expect most estates to stay small.
6. **A civic calendar built for ~100-avatar synchronised instances (R15).** Fortnite's 14 million and VRChat's 158,192 were reached in lobbies of 100 and instances of a few dozen; Second Life's architecture is sufficient, its calendar is missing.
7. **Own the social layer, bridge to Discord (R15, R21).** Voice, text, crews, scheduling, whitelists and age assurance belong in the world; Discord's breach and age-assurance drift are reasons not to rent them, and the Social SDK is a bridge to the graph people already have.
8. **Design for the evergreen loop, not the spike (R8, R13).** Roblox's DAU fell 19% from peak when the viral wave passed and Zepeto's collapsed when schools reopened; publish retention, DAU/MAU and concurrency so the difference is visible.
9. **Let the avatar economy that already exists in (R29).** VRChat's growth engine is BOOTH's 10-billion-yen VRM trade, which VRChat neither built nor taxes; VRM and glTF import and off-platform marketplaces come before an official store.
10. **Price the world to survive at Linden scale (R6, R30).** Roblox keeps about 75% of spend and still lost $935 million in 2024; a Linden-scale world funds a creator pool only if rent and subscriptions carry the platform first.
